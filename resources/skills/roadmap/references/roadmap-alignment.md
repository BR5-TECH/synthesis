# Aligning the plan with the design

## The plan references

The plan references are the HLD and the other documents that the plan answers to. They come with every turn. Use them when the author asks what is missing, when a request changes the order of the work, and when a row rests on the design.

## Gap analysis

1. Take one part of the design at a time, for example one section of the HLD.
2. For each item of that part, find the plan issue that holds it.
3. A **gap** is an item of the design with no issue, an issue that does not match the design, or an issue with no place in the design.
4. Propose the rows that close the gaps of that part in one proposal. Name the section of the design that each row rests on.
5. Take the next part after the author decided.

## Milestones and order

- A milestone holds the work that must be done together for one deliverable. Check that each milestone holds the features that its deliverable needs.
- Work that another item depends on goes in the same milestone or an earlier one.
- When an edit moves an issue, move its children with it, or say why they stay.
- When a milestone holds more work than its deliverable needs, propose to move the extra work to a later milestone.

## When the plan and the design do not agree

Say so, and name both sides. Do not decide alone which one is correct: ask the author when the answer changes the plan.
