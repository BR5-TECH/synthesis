# Comments panel

**Spec code:** `CMP`

## Intent
Vertical-panel surface listing every discussion the project holds, wherever it was opened: a discussion about a passage of an artifact, about a whole artifact, about a draft, about a note, and the conversations of a Flow, a Diff, and a History view. The Editor's comment rail (`CMT-comments.md`) serves the artifact in front of the author; this panel serves the review as a whole, answering "what is still open across everything?" without opening each owner to find out. Discussions are grouped by the state that decides whether they still need someone — **Active**, **Locked**, **Resolved** — and each row carries a **Fragment** or **Whole** badge, the fragment quote where it has one, and the comment that opened it, which is enough to recognise a conversation without reading it. Activating a row calls the one route `revealDiscussion` (`CVP-conversation-presentation.md` CVP-FR-06): an available artifact or draft opens its owner surface with the discussion in it, a note opens a conversation tab, and an owner that no longer resolves opens a fallback conversation tab that names the unavailable owner — so the panel is the index and never the place the conversation is read or continued. Out of scope: being a conversation surface itself, since one conversation has one presentation and this panel is the list of them rather than a second copy of any; writing anything — no thread is opened, no comment posted, and no lock or resolution set from here, because a comment belongs beside the text it is about; an **Orphaned** group, since orphaning is a match of a fragment against an artifact's text (`CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-18) and this panel never reads artifact content; scoping the list to one artifact, which is what the rail already is; and discussions on a historical revision, since a fragment targets the artifact's current version alone.

## User stories
- As a reviewer returning to a project, I want one list of every unresolved discussion in it, so I can tell how much of the review is still open without opening artifacts one at a time.
- As an author working through a review, I want to click a discussion and land on the passage it is about, so answering an objection costs one click rather than a search.
- As an author closing out a review, I want the settled conversations gathered separately from the live ones, so what is left in front of me is what still needs me.
- As a reader, I want to recognise a discussion from its fragment and its opening comment, so I can decide whether it is the one I am looking for before opening anything.

- Layout notes: the filter field is pinned above the scrollable list and stays visible while it scrolls, in the position every vertical panel gives it — directly above the list it narrows (per `SNV-shell-navigation.md` SNV-FR-58); the panel carries no create affordance and no footer. Group headers carry the group's name and its discussion count, and appear in the fixed order Active, Locked, Resolved. A row's owner line is its first line, carrying the owner's label — an artifact's path in the case it has on disk (per `SNV-shell-navigation.md` SNV-FR-57) — and the text badge **Fragment** or **Whole** at its trailing edge; the fragment quote, where the discussion has one, sits below it prefixed by a quote glyph and truncated to one line, and the opening comment's author and relative time share a line above its rendered body, which truncates to a few lines. Where that comment is an agent's and its participant carries a title, the title takes a line of its own between the author line and the body, set quieter than the name and truncated to one line; where it carries none, or an empty one, the row spends no line on it and leaves nothing in its place. That body is typeset at the panel's own UI type scale, not at the document scale of the Editor's page — a row is a row in a narrow panel, and a body set at document size dwarfs every other row in it. It is the same scale the Editor's rail sets a card body at (per `CMT-comments.md`), so a comment is the same size wherever it is read. The reply count is the row's last line and is absent on a discussion of one comment; an attachment count sits at that line's trailing edge and is absent on a discussion whose opening comment attaches nothing, the two sharing the line so neither costs the row a line of its own. A locked row carries a lock marker on its owner line, and a row whose owner has gone carries its **Unresolved** marker there. The whole row is one activation target. Errors from the list operation attach above the list, in place of it.

## UI contract boundary
- **Owned by the UI**: the grouping of discussions into Active, Locked, and Resolved and the precedence that puts each discussion in exactly one of them; the fixed order of the groups and their discussion counts; the ordering of rows within a group; the row's composition — owner, badge, fragment quote, opening comment, reply count, attachment count — and the truncation of each part; the rendering of the opening comment's Markdown body to rich text, of its author as a human or an agent, and of an agent's title line where its participant carries one (CMP-FR-30); relative-timestamp formatting and the hover disclosure of the absolute instant; the filter field and the client-side matching it performs; the call of `revealDiscussion` a row activation performs; the rendering of a discussion whose owner no longer resolves together with the marker that names that state; and the empty state. Where an activation lands is decided by the one route of `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` (CVP-FR-06, CVP-FR-54), and the unread, pending, and failed indicators a row carries are the registry's state rendered rather than anything this panel computes (CVP-FR-37).
- **Delegated to backend (abstract)**: `"list all discussions"` — owned by `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md`, which also owns the discussion and comment records, the participant stamped into every comment, the storage of an attachment's bytes, and the append-only log they persist to. The panel follows that module's `"discussion changed"` event and updates from its payload rather than re-reading (CMP-FR-18). It invokes nothing else: it performs no write, so it never appends a log line and resolves an author identity only to label a local participant's comments (CMT-FR-ZCAE), and it renders no attachment's content, so it never reads one back (CMP-FR-26). Opening an owning artifact reuses the routing the Project panel click-through uses (per `LIB-library.md` LIB-FR-03) and introduces no command of its own.

## Functional requirements
1. **CMP-FR-01** Comments is a vertical-panel surface with its own activity-bar toggle, placed immediately below Notes in the strip's leading cluster (per `SNV-shell-navigation.md` SNV-FR-44). It is not the surface selected when a project opens; the Project panel is (per `OVW-overview.md` OVW-FR-05).
2. **CMP-FR-02** The panel's scope is the whole project: every discussion of every owner the project holds — artifact, draft, and note owners; fragment-targeted and whole-target discussions; Flow, Diff, and History conversations — served by `"list all discussions"`. It carries no scope selector and does not rebind when the active tab changes, so the list a reviewer is working down stays where they left it.
3. **CMP-FR-03** Discussions are gathered into exactly three groups, rendered in the fixed order **Active**, **Locked**, **Resolved**.
4. **CMP-FR-04** Each discussion appears in exactly one group, the first it qualifies for by the precedence **Resolved**, then **Locked**, then **Active**: a resolved discussion is Resolved whether or not it is also locked, an unresolved locked discussion is Locked, and everything else is Active. The three counts therefore sum to the number of discussions in the project.
5. **CMP-FR-05** Each group header names its group and the number of discussions in it. A group holding no discussion is not rendered at all, header included.
6. **CMP-FR-06** Each row shows the owner of the discussion — the project-relative path of an artifact, the name of a draft, or the label of a note, with the origin of a Flow, Diff, or History conversation named beside it — a text badge **Fragment** or **Whole**, the discussion's fragment quote where it has a fragment target, and the discussion's first comment — its author, a compact relative timestamp, and its body rendered as rich text from Markdown on the terms of `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-09. A discussion holding more than one comment additionally shows how many replies follow the first.
7. **CMP-FR-07** A comment's author renders as a human or as an agent distinguishably, from the participant the backend stamped (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-11); the panel never derives an author from anything it holds locally, except the label of CMP-FR-KHUM.
8. **CMP-FR-08** A row's relative timestamp is the discussion's last activity. Hovering it discloses the absolute instant.
9. **CMP-FR-09** Within each group, rows are ordered by last activity, most recent first, across artifacts rather than gathered by artifact.
10. **CMP-FR-10** Activating a row calls `revealDiscussion` with the discussion's stable identity (per `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` CVP-FR-06), so what the activation does depends on the discussion and its owner and never on what the panel is showing. A discussion already shown in a surface is focused there. Otherwise an **available artifact or draft** owner opens its owner surface — the artifact's Editor tab revealed in the Project panel and opened with the routing the Project panel click-through uses (per `LIB-library.md` LIB-FR-03), or the draft's New Artifact tab — with the discussion shown in it; an artifact already open is focused rather than opened a second time. A **note** discussion opens or focuses its conversation tab. An owner that **no longer resolves** opens or focuses a fallback conversation tab that identifies the unavailable owner.
11. **CMP-FR-11** An activation that lands on an owner surface lands on the discussion: for a fragment-targeted discussion the tab is on the surface that renders the rail (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-02) with the rail showing and that discussion's card focused and its fragment scrolled into view, whatever mode the tab was in and whether or not its rail was open (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-36). A resolved discussion lands the same way, its card reached by expanding the rail's resolved disclosure. A whole-target discussion lands in the rail's Discussion section or in the action control's discussion panel (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-53).
    No activation ever creates a second surface, tab, or composer for a discussion: a discussion already shown is focused and never opened again, and one with no surface gets exactly one (per `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` CVP-FR-02, CVP-FR-52). The panel renders no conversation of its own on any of these routes — it is the index, and the surface the activation lands on is where the conversation is read.
12. **CMP-FR-12** A discussion whose artifact owner no longer resolves in the project renders with its last-known project-relative path and carries an **Unresolved** marker on its owner line — the same word and the same treatment the Notes panel gives a note whose entity has gone (per `NTS-notes.md` NTS-FR-23), so one state reads one way wherever the window shows it. The marker names the **owner**, not the conversation: a discussion's own settled-or-open state is what the groups of CMP-FR-03 carry, and the two are independent, so an Unresolved row appears in whichever of Active, Locked, and Resolved its stored state puts it. Such a row stays an activation target: it opens the fallback conversation tab that identifies the unavailable owner (CMP-FR-10). It is never hidden and never discarded, so a conversation outlives the file it was about (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-25).
13. **CMP-FR-13** A discussion the rail would render as orphaned (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-19) is grouped by its stored lock and resolution state like any other, because deciding otherwise would mean reading every commented artifact's text and the panel reads none.
14. **CMP-FR-14** The panel carries a text filter field that narrows the rendered list to the discussions whose fragment quote, first comment's body, first comment's attachment names, or owner label matches the entered text; a whole-target discussion, having no fragment quote, is matched on the rest. Matching is performed client-side over the discussions already loaded, case-insensitively, and requires no backend call. A group left with no matching discussion is hidden, and the counts in the remaining headers describe what is rendered.
15. **CMP-FR-15** The filter text is held in memory for the session: it survives switching to another panel surface and back and any number of tab changes, and it is empty when the application launches.
16. **CMP-FR-16** The panel is read-only. No row, header, or control anywhere in it opens a discussion, posts a comment, sets a lock or a resolution, quotes a message, offers an overflow menu, or renders a second copy of a conversation; every write to a discussion happens in whichever surface currently shows it (per `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` CVP-FR-33), a fragment-targeted discussion is begun in the Editor rail (`CMT-comments.md`), and a whole-target discussion is begun from a tab's action control (`ACT-action-control.md`) and never from here. Continuing a conversation this panel lists is reached by activating its row and working in the surface that lands (CMP-FR-10).
17. **CMP-FR-17** The panel renders the project's discussions in full even when no identity resolves, unlike the rail's composers (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-24), because reading a conversation is not attributed to anyone. It reads `"resolve comment author identity"` for display alone and never opens a picker or states a missing token.
18. **CMP-FR-KHUM** A row whose comments carry the local participant renders their author under the current project identity or **Me**, on the terms of `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-ZCAE, and refreshes the label on `"github tokens changed"` without re-reading the list. Any other participant renders as stamped.
18. **CMP-FR-18** The panel invokes `"list all discussions"` when it becomes the vertical panel's active surface and follows the `"discussion changed"` event thereafter, updating in place the discussion each payload names — a discussion opened, a comment posted, a lock or resolution set, and an agent's answer landing alike (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-QTNB) — and moving it between Active, Locked, and Resolved when the write changed which group it belongs to (CMP-FR-04). A discussion the panel has not seen before joins its group from the same payload. It re-reads for none of this, so the groups agree with what the author has just done and with what has just been said to them without the panel asking.
19. **CMP-FR-19** Neither the panel nor the module behind it watches the comment logs: a discussion a different process appends to becomes visible on the panel's next read rather than announcing itself (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-31).
20. **CMP-FR-20** When the project's active worktree changes (per `OVW-overview.md` OVW-FR-12), the panel holds nothing across the switch and its next read serves the same logs it served before it, the store being the repository's rather than the worktree's (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-28).
21. **CMP-FR-21** A project holding no discussion renders the first-class empty state of `SNV-shell-navigation.md` SNV-FR-60 in place of the groups — a centred block stating that no discussion has been opened and naming the Editor's comment rail and the Discuss controls as where one is begun (per `CMT-comments.md`) — carrying no action, because the panel is read-only (CMP-FR-16). No group header is rendered behind it.
22. **CMP-FR-22** The panel opens no floating overlay of its own — it has no menu, no picker, and no confirmation step — so it neither dismisses nor is dismissed by the mutually-exclusive overlays of the main window (per `STB-status-bar.md` STB-FR-12).
23. **CMP-FR-23** The panel is not tabbable and cannot be dragged into the main viewport (per `TAB-tabs.md` TAB-FR-03).
24. **CMP-FR-24** A filter matching no discussion in a project that holds discussions is not the empty state of CMP-FR-21: it renders a stated message in the list's own region with the filter field still present and still holding what was typed (per `SNV-shell-navigation.md` SNV-FR-61), and no group header behind it. The author's next move differs between the two — opening a discussion in an artifact, or changing the text in front of them.
25. **CMP-FR-25** A row whose discussion's opening comment carries attachments states how many, so a reviewer scanning the list can tell which conversations have a picture in them. A comment attaching nothing carries no marker at all rather than a count of none.
26. **CMP-FR-26** The panel renders no attachment's content: no thumbnail, no preview, and no linked image is loaded anywhere in it, and `"read comment attachment"` is invoked nowhere. A row reports that an attachment exists and the rail is where it is looked at (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-48), on the same principle that keeps the panel from reading artifact content.
27. **CMP-FR-27** A **whole-target** row carries no fragment quote, there being no passage it is pinned to, and states that it is a whole-owner discussion in that quote's place, so a row is never read as a discussion whose fragment failed to load. Everything else it carries is what any row carries — the owner, the badge, the opening comment, the reply count, the attachment count, and its group. Draft, note, Flow, Diff, and History discussions reach this panel on the same terms as artifact discussions, each with its own owner label (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-40).
28. **CMP-FR-28** A row carries its conversation's **unread**, **pending-response**, and **failed-response** indicators, read from that discussion's registry record rather than computed here (per `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` CVP-FR-37), so a row and a conversation tab never disagree about which conversation is waiting on the author. The failed indicator says that an agent could not answer there and that the offer to ask it again is still standing (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-RUVS), which is exactly what a project-wide index of a review is for: a conversation the author addressed an agent in and never got an answer from is otherwise indistinguishable, from here, from one nobody has replied to yet. It says nothing about what failed and offers no way to retry from this panel — retrying is done where the conversation is read (CMP-FR-16), which the row's own activation is the route to. A discussion with no registry record carries none of the three, there being no reading of it for anything to be unread against. The indicators are carried by more than colour and change nothing else about the row: its group, its ordering, and its filtering are exactly what CMP-FR-04, CMP-FR-09, and CMP-FR-14 make them.
29. **CMP-FR-29** A row whose owner no longer resolves is activated like any other row (CMP-FR-10): it opens the fallback conversation tab, which renders the discussion with its unavailable-owner state naming what is missing (per `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` CVP-FR-45), and the conversation is still read and written to there. The row keeps its **Unresolved** marker, is never hidden, and is never discarded.
30. **CMP-FR-30** A row whose opening comment is an agent's and whose participant carries a **non-empty title** renders that title on a line of its own between the author line and the body, on exactly the terms the Editor's rail renders one (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-KFUF), so the same comment reads the same way in the panel and in the card. It is the snapshot that comment's participant carries and never the agent's current title, this panel resolving no agent record and holding no roster to resolve one from. A participant carrying no title, or an empty one, gives the row no such line and nothing in its place — no placeholder and never the words `Not defined`.

## Non-functional requirements
- The panel is a narrow vertical surface: a long fragment quote truncates to one line and a long opening comment to a few lines, rather than widening the panel or letting one discussion fill it.
- Filtering (CMP-FR-14) runs against the already-loaded discussion set and never issues a backend call, so typing in the filter field never waits on the backend.
- Rendering the panel costs one list call regardless of how many artifacts the project has comments on; a project with no comments costs that one call and no rendering.
- The panel never reads an artifact's content and never loads an attachment's, so the cost of a read is bounded by the number of commented artifacts rather than by their size or by the pictures attached to them.
- The panel never asks the backend what it has already been told: after the first read, a project whose review is being actively worked on costs one event per write and no further call.
- Relative timestamps refresh as time passes without the user reopening the panel.
- Every row is focusable and activatable by keyboard alone, as is the filter field, so the panel is navigable without a pointer.
