# Comments

**Spec code:** `CMT`

## Intent
The discussion surface of the Editor for **fragment-targeted discussions**: the conversations about ranges of the open file's text. An author selects a passage and starts a discussion about it. The discussion is listed in the **discussion column** that stands beside the document (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-KTVW), and the passage it is about stays marked in the text, so a reader sees which paragraph is under discussion and moves between the passage and its discussion in one action. The column is the same one a draft is discussed in, so a conversation about a file and a conversation about a draft look and work the same. An artifact carries as many discussions as its author needs; each is a conversation whose comments are written and rendered as Markdown, each is **resolved** when the matter it raised is closed and then takes no further comment until it is reopened, and each comment can quote an earlier message in its own discussion so a long exchange stays legible. A comment carries pictures as well as prose — a screenshot dropped onto the composer, or a link to one already hosted — because the fastest way to say what is wrong with a passage is often to show it. Comments are attributed to the GitHub account the project's credential authenticates as, and discussions persist in the project itself as an append-only log (`../core/CMS-comments-storage.md`), so a review survives a relaunch and travels with the repository to everyone who checks it out. Not every participant is a person: an agent the project has enrolled answers in the discussion it was addressed in, with a comment of its own, so a conversation with a collaborator who is not human is read, quoted, and resolved exactly like one with a colleague. How such a collaborator is summoned, who a message reaches, and what its card shows while an answer is being composed are `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md`'s; what this surface owns is the discussion that holds the exchange. The column also holds the **whole-target discussions** of the open item — conversations about the whole of the file, or in a New Artifact tab about the whole of the draft, rather than about a passage of it — listed with the fragment-targeted ones, begun from the column's opening composer, and read, answered, and routed on exactly the terms a fragment-targeted discussion is. Every discussion renders the shared discussion surface and composer of `CVP-conversation-presentation.md`, so a conversation looks and behaves the same here as in every other place it is shown; no discussion offers to move its discussion into another presentation mode, because there are none. Out of scope: an agent's part in a conversation — the mention that summons one, the rules that decide who a comment reaches, the pending and failed contributions, and the notice an omitted image raises — all of which are `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md`'s; where a discussion is revealed from outside the column, which `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` decides; the column's grid, splitter, split presets, hide control, and transcript layout, which are `DDS-draft-discussion.md`'s; editing or deleting a posted comment, since a discussion that has outlived its usefulness is resolved rather than rewritten; addressing a comment to a particular **person**, an agent alone being addressed by a tag and routed by one; selecting a passage to comment on in raw-text mode or in a historical revision; seeing a comment another process appends arrive without reopening the artifact, nothing here watching the logs; listing the discussions of any artifact but the open one, since this column serves the file in front of the author and the project-wide list is the Comments panel's surface (`CMP-comments-panel.md`); and GitHub's own pull-request review comments, which the Git panel renders (per `GIT-git.md` GIT-FR-05) and which have nothing to do with these.

## User stories
- As a reviewer, I want to select a paragraph and say what is wrong with it, so my objection is tied to the thing it is about rather than kept in a separate document.
- As an author reading a review, I want to go from a discussion to the passage it concerns and back in one action, so I can work down the artifact answering objections in order.
- As a participant in a long discussion, I want to quote the specific line I am answering, so a reply three messages later is still unambiguous.
- As an author, I want to mark a discussion resolved and have it leave the list of open discussions, so the discussions still in front of me are the ones that still need me.
- As an author closing out a review, I want a resolved discussion to take no further comment, so nothing is added to a decision already made.

Its chooser holds one tab for each unresolved discussion — whole-target and fragment-targeted alike — then the control that begins another discussion, then the resolved disclosure. Fragment ranges are highlighted in the rich body and on a source surface, each with an underline as well as a background, and the focused discussion's range is highlighted distinctly. An orphaned discussion keeps its tab and renders its quote in the unavailable state. The split presets, the accelerator hint, and the control that hides and shows the column ride at the trailing end of the Editor's toolbar.

- Layout notes: the card's header carries the discussion's opener, the discussion's relative last-activity time, and the overflow button, with the fragment's quoted text on a second line truncated to one line. Comments are stacked oldest first, each with its author and relative time on one line above its rendered body; an agent's comment is distinguished from a human's in that line, and one whose participant carries a title takes a second line for it directly beneath, set quieter than the name and above the body. A comment whose participant carries no title, or an empty one, spends no line on it and leaves nothing in its place. A comment's attachments sit below its body, an image as a thumbnail bounded to the card's measure and anything else as a named chip. A pending contribution takes the shape of a comment and reads one short line of activity status — **Thinking…** while the agent waits on its model, and the status of the tool it is using while a tool call is active — its cancel control on its author line where a delivered comment carries none. A failed contribution takes the same shape and the same position, distinguished from both a delivered comment and a pending one by its own marker and by the wording of its body, and carries a **Retry** control on its own line below that body — the one control in a card that is lettered rather than a glyph, because it is an offer the author has to recognise rather than an action they already know is there. The composer states who an untagged reply reaches in its **placeholder** rather than on a line of its own: an empty field reads `Reply to @arch, @sec…` where the conversation has active agents and `Reply…` where it has none, a list too long for the field clipped with an ellipsis at its trailing edge rather than wrapped, and nothing above, beside, or below the field states a recipient anywhere. The composer's pending attachments occupy a strip between the text area and the control row, each removable in place. A body is typeset at the card's own UI type scale rather than at the document scale of a page — a card is a narrow box, and it is the scale `CMP-comments-panel.md` sets a comment at too, so a comment is the same size whether it is read in a card or in that panel. A quoted message renders as an attributed block above the body of the comment that quotes it. Each comment carries a Quote control on its header line. The composer is pinned to the card's bottom with Cancel leading and Post trailing; both are glyphs rather than words, as is Quote, so a narrow card spends its width on the conversation. The composer is absent entirely on a resolved discussion. Errors returned by a backend operation attach inline at the bottom of the card that triggered them.

## UI contract boundary
- **Owned by the UI**: which discussions the discussion column lists for the open item and in which order, the column's grid, splitter, presets, hide control, and transcript layout being `DDS-draft-discussion.md`'s; the selection affordance that starts a discussion and the opening composer it opens for the fragment; the mapping of a rich-body selection onto a character range in the artifact's Markdown source; the tracking of every fragment's offsets against the in-memory buffer as the author edits; the re-anchor search performed when an artifact loads and the marking of a discussion as orphaned when it fails; the rendering of comment bodies from Markdown to rich text and the plain-Markdown composer that authors them; the title line an agent comment carries below its author line and the absence of one where its participant carries no title (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-KFUF, CTA-FR-KYPK); the Quote action and the seeding of a composer from it; the card's overflow menu and its one resolve entry; the unavailable state of an orphaned discussion, the resolved disclosure, and which discussions it holds; the derivation of a conversation's active agents from its own human comments and the project's current roster, the recipients an empty composer names in its placeholder, and the dispatch rules that follow from both; relative-timestamp formatting; the attach affordance, the pending attachments a composer holds and their removal before posting, the encoding of a chosen file into the payload an append carries, and the rendering of a posted comment's attachments as thumbnails, chips, and — for a proposal — a control (CMT-FR-48); the dispatching of a turn for each agent a posted comment addresses, the pending contribution an outstanding turn renders in its discussion together with the activity status it reads, the fixed wording of every status and the fallback one, the rule that picks which of a turn's active tool calls is spoken for, and the control that cancels one; the failed contribution a recoverable terminal failure renders in that same position, its wording, its distinction from a delivered comment and from a pending one, the labelled **Retry** control it carries, and the withholding of that control on a resolved discussion (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-XZUO); and the resolved disclosure's state, which is part of the artifact's retained edit state (per `EDT-editor.md` EDT-FR-28) and so is held only in memory. The column's hidden state and split ratio per file are `DDS-draft-discussion.md`'s. The shared discussion surface and composer a discussion renders, the unread indicator, and the composer accelerator that posts without the pointer are owned by `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` and introduce no operation here.
- **Delegated to backend (abstract)**: `"list discussions for target"`, `"open discussion (target, fragment target, body, attachments)"`, `"add comment to discussion (discussion, body, quotes, attachments)"`, `"set discussion resolution"`, `"reanchor discussion fragment"`, `"read comment attachment"`, and `"resolve comment author identity"` — all owned by `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md`, which also owns the discussion and comment records, the participant identity stamped into every comment, the storage of an attachment's bytes, and the append-only log they persist to. The column follows that module's `"discussion changed"` event and redraws from its payload rather than re-reading (CMT-FR-04). The same operation names serve a draft's files for a draft owner (CMS-FR-36), so the column invokes one set of names and the module resolves where the discussions live. Opening a discussion is `"open discussion (target, fragment target, body, attachments)"`, invoked from the column's opening composer (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-VHZN, DDS-FR-QMBC); every later comment in one is posted through the same operations any discussion's are. The UI never composes an author identity of its own and never writes a log line. When `"resolve comment author identity"` reports that a token must be selected, the token picker modal of `GHA-github-authentication.md` (GHA-FR-16) is what resolves it, and when it reports that none is stored it resolves the local participant **Me**, so the column shows no picker and no route; neither introduces a command here. The column follows `"github tokens changed"`, owned by `../core/GTS-github-token-storage.md` (GTS-FR-AEQO), to refresh the identity and the labels of CMT-FR-ZCAE. The agent-turn operations the column invokes, the `"agent turn state changed"` event it follows, and the reading of `"list project agents"` a tag resolves against belong to `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md`, which declares them. The mention picker those agents are offered through, and the tag syntax that addresses one, are owned by `AGT-agents.md` and introduce no operation here. The state a `proposal` attachment's control renders is read through `"list draft change proposals"` and kept current by the `"draft change proposals changed"` event, both owned by `../core/DCP-draft-change-proposals.md`; the state a `prompt_proposal` attachment's control renders is read through `"list prompt change proposals"` and kept current by the `"prompt change proposals changed"` event, both owned by `../core/PCP-prompt-change-proposals.md`. The modals those controls open, and every operation that reads or decides a proposal, are `DCR-draft-change-review.md`'s and `PCR-prompt-change-review.md`'s and introduce nothing here. A discussion's pending question set is read through `"read discussion question set"`, kept current by the `"discussion question set changed"` event, and answered through `"submit discussion question answers"`, all owned by `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` and all invoked as `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` declares (DQA-FR-JWEF, DQA-FR-KDVU); the block that renders them introduces nothing here.

## Functional requirements
1. **CMT-FR-01** The discussions of the Editor tab's file render in the **discussion column** of `DDS-draft-discussion.md` (DDS-FR-KTVW), which stands beside the document column and is separated from it by the splitter. That region — named the **column** throughout this spec — is part of the tab, not a floating overlay of the main window: showing it closes none of the mutually-exclusive overlays and none of them closes it (per `STB-status-bar.md` STB-FR-12). The column covers no part of the page, so a comment is never read through the sentence it is about.
2. **CMT-FR-02** The column renders in the Editor tab on every surface the Editor sets a file's text on: the **WYSIWYG** surface and the **raw-text** surface of a Markdown file (per `EDT-editor.md` EDT-FR-17), and the single source surface of a source file (per `ESH-editor-source-files.md` ESH-FR-SSDV). A switch between the two modes of a Markdown file leaves the column, the discussion it reads, and every discussion as they stand. Fragment highlights and the Comment affordance render on the WYSIWYG surface and on a source surface. The raw-text surface renders neither, and toggling back renders the same discussions against the same fragments.
3. **CMT-FR-03** The column is available on every artifact the Editor opens, on a plain text file whichever of the two surfaces it is edited on (per `ESH-editor-source-files.md` ESH-FR-ATDS, ESH-FR-BLTT), and on every file of a draft in a New Artifact tab (CMT-FR-37), each discussion's fragment targeting the file's source rather than anything an artifact type or a project location supplies.
4. **CMT-FR-04** On opening an artifact the Editor invokes `"list discussions for target"` once with the artifact's target and renders what it returns in the column: the fragment-targeted and the whole-target discussions alike, in the order of CMT-FR-53. Thereafter the column follows the `"discussion changed"` event and redraws the discussion the payload names from the payload itself — a comment posted in this column, one posted from another tab or from a second window, a resolution set or cleared anywhere, and an agent's answer arriving alike (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-QTNB) — so it neither re-reads nor polls for any of them. A discussion a different process appends to becomes visible when the artifact is next opened, nothing watching the logs (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-31).
5. **CMT-FR-05** Selecting a non-empty range of text on a surface that renders fragment highlights (CMT-FR-02) — a rich body, or a source file's source surface — reveals a **Comment** affordance at the end of the selection: a single graphical control beside the passage rather than a filled block over it. Activating it shows the column where it is hidden and opens the column's **opening composer** for that fragment, with focus in its message field (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-QMBC). A selection of zero length reveals nothing.
6. **CMT-FR-06** A discussion's **fragment** is a character range in the artifact's **source** — the Markdown of a Markdown file, the file's own text otherwise — together with the exact source substring that range covers. A selection made on a source surface is already such a range, and the UI maps a rich-body selection onto one, so a fragment means the same passage on whichever surface the file is edited on. A selection lying within one block is found in the source as it stands; a selection spanning blocks is located by its two ends — the first and last rendered lines it covers, the last searched for after the first — and the range between them is taken from the source with the intervening Markdown syntax the rich surface hides included, so an ordinary selection across a heading and the text under it fragments like any other. A selection neither route can place — including one whose last line lies nowhere after its first — opens no discussion.
   - *Why:* Targeting a smaller or a different passage than the author chose is worse than not targeting a fragment at all.
7. **CMT-FR-07** Posting the opening composer of a fragment invokes `"open discussion (target, fragment target, body, attachments)"` with the file's target and the selection's fragment target, and the column then reads the returned discussion, with focus in its reply composer. Discarding that composer invokes nothing and leaves no discussion behind.
8. **CMT-FR-08** A discussion card stacks its comments oldest first, each showing its author, a compact relative timestamp, and its body rendered as rich text. Hovering a timestamp discloses the comment's absolute instant.
9. **CMT-FR-09** A comment is authored as **Markdown source** in a plain composer and rendered as rich text once posted, so emphasis, code spans, lists, and links in a comment read as themselves in the card.
10. **CMT-FR-10** A comment's author renders as a human or as an agent distinguishably, from the participant the backend stamped (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-11); the UI never derives an author from anything it holds locally. The one label that is not the stamped one is the local participant's (CMT-FR-ZCAE).
11. **CMT-FR-11** A card whose discussion is not resolved carries a composer at its foot. Posting invokes `"add comment to discussion (discussion, body, quotes, attachments)"` and appends the returned comment to the card. Cancelling closes the composer and discards its content without invoking anything.
12. **CMT-FR-12** Every comment in a card carrying a composer (CMT-FR-11) carries a **Quote** action. Activating it seeds the card's composer with an attributed quote of that comment: the part of it the reader has selected, or the whole message when they have selected none of it. A selection lying outside that comment — elsewhere in the artifact, or in another message of the same discussion — is not what is being quoted and is disregarded in favour of the whole message. A composer may carry several quotes, of several different comments in the same discussion, and each may be removed before posting.
13. **CMT-FR-13** A quote is carried structurally in the `quotes` of the posted comment rather than only as text in its body, and renders in the card as an attributed block above the quoting comment's own body, naming the quoted comment's author. Activating it brings the quoted comment into view within the card.
14. **CMT-FR-14** Comments are immutable once posted. No card, menu, or composer anywhere in the column offers to edit or delete a comment or a discussion; a discussion that has outlived its usefulness is resolved (CMT-FR-16).
15. **CMT-FR-15** The card's overflow menu carries exactly one entry. It reads **Mark resolved** on an active discussion and **Reopen discussion** on a resolved one, and it invokes `"set discussion resolution"`. A discussion stored as locked reads as resolved (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-WKZR), so its entry reads **Reopen discussion**.
16. **CMT-FR-16** A resolved card states that it is resolved in words, carries no composer and no Quote action, and keeps its comments fully readable. The backend refuses a comment to it from the author and from an agent alike (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-17). **Reopen discussion** restores the composer and the Quote action and dispatches no agent turn.
17. **CMT-FR-17** A resolved discussion leaves the chooser's discussion tabs and is counted in a **resolved disclosure** at the foot of the chooser (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md`). Expanding it lists the resolved discussions, and each one reads in full in the column; reopening one returns it to its place among the open discussions. The disclosure is collapsed by default and its state is per artifact.
18. **CMT-FR-18** When an artifact loads, each stored fragment is resolved against the loaded Markdown source by searching for the fragment's stored quote nearest to its stored offsets. A discussion whose quote is found is placed at the found range; a discussion whose quote is not found anywhere is **orphaned**.
19. **CMT-FR-19** An orphaned discussion keeps its tab in the column's chooser and renders the quote it was targeted at in the **unavailable** state in place of a highlight (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-QMBC). It is fully readable, still carries its overflow menu, and still accepts comments while it is not resolved. It is never hidden and never discarded.
20. **CMT-FR-20** While the artifact is edited, every fragment's offsets track the buffer: text inserted or removed before a fragment moves it, and an edit that destroys the targeted text orphans that discussion for the rest of the session. Fragment tracking is not an edit — it marks the artifact dirty no more than reading it does and occupies no position in the undo history (per `EDT-editor.md` EDT-FR-23).
21. **CMT-FR-21** When the artifact is saved and a discussion's resolved fragment differs from the one the backend holds, the column invokes `"reanchor discussion fragment"` with the current range and quote. A discussion whose fragment is unchanged is not reanchored, and no reanchor is invoked for an orphaned discussion.
22. **CMT-FR-22** Choosing **Load from filesystem** on the external-change modal (per `EXC-editor-external-change.md` EXC-FR-WDAV) re-resolves every fragment against the newly loaded content on the terms of CMT-FR-18, so a discussion whose passage the other writer removed becomes orphaned rather than pointing at unrelated text.
23. **CMT-FR-23** A discussion whose fragment lies inside the WYSIWYG frontmatter region while that region is collapsed expands the region as a peek when that discussion is focused in the column, on the same principle as an undo that reaches into it (per `EDT-editor.md` EDT-FR-25).
24. **CMT-FR-24** The column invokes `"resolve comment author identity"` when it first renders for a project. The Comment affordance (CMT-FR-05) and every composer are enabled while the answer is a participant, a GitHub account or **Me**. While the call is refused, they are disabled and the column states the reason.
25. **CMT-FR-25** An identity refused because a token must be selected opens the token picker modal (per `GHA-github-authentication.md` GHA-FR-16); confirming it re-resolves the identity and enables commenting, and cancelling leaves the column disabled with the reason stated.
26. **CMT-FR-26** A project that stores no token resolves **Me** (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-HTOA): the column opens no picker, renders no missing-token error, and offers no route to Global settings. A refusal because GitHub is unreachable or the keychain is unavailable is rendered as its own distinct reason, because the two call for different responses from the author.
27. **CMT-FR-ZCAE** A comment by the local participant renders as a human. Its label is the GitHub login that `"resolve comment author identity"` returns now, in the label of an ordinary GitHub-authored comment. While that call returns **Me** or a refusal, including a required token binding, the label is **Me**. It refreshes on `"github tokens changed"` and rewrites no stored comment.
28. **CMT-FR-28** Every fragment-targeted discussion that is open shows its fragment in the owner's text surface, and the focused discussion's fragment is highlighted distinctly from the rest. A fragment highlight never relies on colour alone: it carries an underline in addition to its background, and an accessible label naming the discussion it belongs to, with a stronger underline and label state on the focused one. Activating a highlight shows the column where it is hidden and focuses that discussion in it. Activating a discussion's fragment quote in the column scrolls its fragment into view; in raw-text mode it first puts the tab on the WYSIWYG surface, because the raw-text surface renders no highlight (CMT-FR-02).
29. **CMT-FR-29** The Editor tab's toolbar carries the control that hides and shows the column at its trailing end, with the split presets and the accelerator hint leading of it while the column shows (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-XQMF, DDS-FR-PNXR). The control stands on every surface of the tab, both editing modes of a Markdown file included, so a switch to raw text never hides the way to the discussions. Each unresolved discussion, orphaned discussions included, has its own tab in the column's chooser (CMT-FR-53).
30. **CMT-FR-30** The column's hidden state and split ratio are kept per file and restored when the file opens again, in the same session and after a relaunch (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-PNXR, DDS-FR-XQMF). A file whose column the author has never hidden shows it. The resolved disclosure's state belongs to the artifact and is part of its retained edit state (per `EDT-editor.md` EDT-FR-28): it survives the tab closing and reopening within the session, is held only in memory, and is discarded with that state.
31. **CMT-FR-31** Nothing the column renders is part of the editing surface: its text contributes no match to the Find and Find & Replace panels (per `EFR-editor-find-replace.md` EFR-FR-DDUX), a replacement never rewrites it, and no action taken in it occupies a position in the artifact's undo history (per `EDT-editor.md` EDT-FR-23).
32. **CMT-FR-32** The column's own transient surfaces — a discussion's overflow menu and the Comment affordance — are anchored within the tab and at most one is open at a time; opening one dismisses any other, and each dismisses on Escape or an outside click without invoking any operation.
33. **CMT-FR-33** While the external-change modal blocks the tab (per `EXC-editor-external-change.md` EXC-FR-VTUH), the column is inert along with every other operation: no discussion is opened, no comment is posted, and no resolution is set or cleared until the author resolves the modal.
34. **CMT-FR-34** A failed operation renders its typed error inline at the foot of the card that triggered it, leaving the composer's content intact so a post is retried without retyping. A comment refused because the discussion is resolved (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-17) re-renders the card as resolved.
35. **CMT-FR-35** A card's own controls — Post, Cancel, Quote, removing a pending quote — are graphical rather than lettered, because a card is narrow and a row of words costs the comment the width it needs. Each carries the name it would have spelled out, so it stays reachable and operable by that name without a pointer.
36. **CMT-FR-36** A discussion reached from the Comments panel (per `CMP-comments-panel.md` CMP-FR-10) is revealed through the one route `revealDiscussion` (per `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` CVP-FR-06). For a discussion on an available artifact it lands in that artifact's Editor tab: the column is shown and that discussion is focused in it. For a fragment-targeted discussion the tab is also put on a surface that renders highlights (CMT-FR-02) — WYSIWYG for a Markdown file, and for a source file the one surface it has — and its fragment is scrolled into view (CMT-FR-28), whatever mode the tab was in and whether or not its column was hidden. A resolved discussion is reached by expanding the resolved disclosure (CMT-FR-17). The column's shown state for that file is thereafter kept on the same terms as any other change to it (CMT-FR-30). A discussion whose owner is unavailable is reached in the fallback conversation tab rather than here (per `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` CVP-FR-54).
37. **CMT-FR-37** The same discussion column renders in a New Artifact tab beside a **draft's prompt** (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-KTVW, `NAW-new-artifact.md` NAW-FR-14). Every requirement above holds there unchanged — the selection affordance, the fragment targeting, the composers, the resolve entry, the unavailable state, the resolved disclosure, and the addressing of an agent — because a draft's prompt is Markdown like any artifact, and a review of one should not have to be learned a second time. Four things differ, all owned elsewhere: the discussions are served for a draft owner rather than an artifact owner by the same operations (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-36); they are deleted with the draft rather than outliving it (per `NAW-new-artifact.md` NAW-FR-20); a turn dispatched from there names the draft as its target, which is what gives the agent the draft's prompt as the material under discussion (per `../core/AGC-agent-conversations.md` AGC-FR-07); and the whole-target discussions are the draft's rather than the prompt's, so they stay in the column while a past version is being read in that tab (CMT-FR-53, CMT-FR-54, per `NAW-new-artifact.md` NAW-FR-14). The draft tab's own additions — the hunk discussions, the subject strip, and the anchor bar — are `DDS-draft-discussion.md`'s. The floating action control is not among the differences — every tab carrying this column carries one (per `ACT-action-control.md` ACT-FR-01), and a discussion's overflow menu is one of the tab's transient surfaces alongside that control in each of them (per `ACT-action-control.md` ACT-FR-12).
39. **CMT-FR-45** A composer carries an **Attach** control offering exactly two entries and no third: attaching a **file**, which opens the OS file picker and carries the chosen file's bytes, and attaching a **link**, which takes an address and carries it as a reference. Nothing in any composer attaches a `proposal`, which reaches a comment only by an agent proposing one (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-60) and is therefore never something the author is offered a way to compose. Dropping a file onto the composer and pasting image data into it both attach as a file without the control being opened, because the fastest way to show a passage's problem is to paste the screenshot already on the clipboard. A resolved discussion carries no composer (CMT-FR-16) and therefore has no way to attach anything.
40. **CMT-FR-46** Attachments chosen but not yet posted render as a **pending strip** between the composer's text area and its control row, each entry naming what it holds and carrying a control that removes it. A pending attachment is not sent anywhere until the comment is posted, so removing one invokes nothing, and cancelling the composer discards the strip with the typed body (CMT-FR-11).
41. **CMT-FR-47** Posting carries the strip's entries as the operation's `attachments`: a file as its media type, filename, and encoded bytes, and a link as its address and media type. A comment with an empty strip carries an empty list, which is the ordinary case.
42. **CMT-FR-48** A posted comment's attachments render below its body: an image as a thumbnail bounded to the measure of the surface that renders it, so it never widens a card or the column's content column (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-ZMXQ), and anything else as a named chip carrying its filename and media type. Activating either opens it at full size beyond the card, because a thumbnail is an index of the picture rather than the picture. A **reference** attachment renders as neither: it holds no content to show, so it takes the form of a **control** naming the prompt the change was proposed against, which reveals that change. What it reveals is decided by the attachment's own kind and by nothing else: a `proposal` (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-60) names a draft's prompt and scrolls that draft's document column to the first undecided change (per `DCR-draft-change-review.md` DCR-FR-HVXK); a `proposal_hunk` (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-TQVB) names **one** change of a draft proposal and scrolls that column to it; and a `prompt_proposal` (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-66) names a prompt artifact of the project and opens the review of `PCR-prompt-change-review.md` (PCR-FR-03), which opens or focuses that artifact's Editor tab first where it is not already open (PCR-FR-18). The two controls are rendered alike, name their file alike, and are activated alike; the column neither derives the target from the path nor keeps a second treatment for one of them. It is the one attachment whose rendering is an action rather than a preview, because what it names is a decision the author has to make rather than a picture they have to see.
43. **CMT-FR-49** A stored attachment's content is fetched through `"read comment attachment"` and a linked one from its own address, in both cases only when the card holding it is rendered, so an artifact whose review carries many images costs nothing for the discussions the author is not looking at. Content that cannot be loaded — a link that does not resolve, a stored attachment the operation refuses — renders as a chip stating that it could not be loaded and naming what it points at, rather than as an empty frame. A `proposal` attachment fetches nothing at any point: its control renders from what the attachment itself carries, and the proposal's own text is read only where a review renders it (per `DCR-draft-change-review.md` DCR-FR-08), so a conversation carrying a proposal costs the column exactly what one carrying a sentence costs.
44. **CMT-FR-50** An attachment is immutable with the comment that carries it, on the same terms as the comment's own body (CMT-FR-14): no card, chip, or menu anywhere in the column offers to remove, replace, or re-order an attachment once its comment is posted.
45. **CMT-FR-51** An append refused for its attachments — a media type the backend does not accept, a file beyond its size bound, content it could not decode — renders its typed error inline at the foot of the card on the terms of CMT-FR-34, leaving the composer's body and every entry of its pending strip intact, so the offending attachment is removed and the comment posted without any of it being retyped.
46. **CMT-FR-52** A **discussion** is a conversation about an owner: a file of the project, a draft, or a note. It optionally carries a **fragment target**, a range of the owner's text it is about (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-53). A discussion with a fragment target marks that fragment in the owner's text (CMT-FR-28); a discussion without one is about the whole owner. A conversation about the whole and a conversation about a line of it are the same kind of record, rendered by the same shared surface (CMT-FR-HQNV), in the same column. A whole-target discussion is begun from the column's opening composer, which the tab's **Discuss** focuses (per `ACT-action-control.md` ACT-FR-BRZK), and read and continued here.
47. **CMT-FR-53** The column's chooser lists the item's unresolved discussions — whole-target and fragment-targeted alike — in the order they were opened (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-VHZN). Which discussions those are is the tab's item (per `ACT-action-control.md` ACT-FR-02): the open file's in an Editor tab, and the draft's in a New Artifact tab, whose whole-target discussions are about the draft rather than about the file in front of the author. An item carrying no discussion renders no chooser, and the column shows its opening composer under the statement `DDS-draft-discussion.md` sets for that state.
48. **CMT-FR-54** The column invokes `"list discussions for target"` once when the tab opens, with the tab's owner target, and follows the `"discussion changed"` event thereafter on exactly the terms it follows it for a fragment-targeted discussion (CMT-FR-04), so a discussion redraws from the payload of the write that changed it and the column re-reads for none of it. In a New Artifact tab, selecting another file of the draft shows the fragment-targeted discussions whose fragment lies in that file and leaves the whole-target discussions as they stand, they belonging to the draft rather than to the selection; the selection causes no further read.
49. **CMT-FR-55** A whole-target discussion is an ordinary discussion in every respect that does not depend on a fragment: its comments stack oldest first, its composer posts through `"add comment to discussion (discussion, body, quotes, attachments)"`, its comments are quotable and immutable, it attaches files and links, it carries the same overflow menu with the same resolve entry, its composer names its recipients in its placeholder, it renders pending contributions reading the same activity statuses, and it surfaces a failed operation inline (CMT-FR-08 through CMT-FR-16, CMT-FR-34, per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-ZOLW, CMT-FR-45 through CMT-FR-51, CTA-FR-IAKP, CTA-FR-FBJR, CTA-FR-IWOJ). It carries no fragment quote, highlights nothing in the body, is never orphaned (CMT-FR-18), and never moves its fragment (CMT-FR-21).
50. **CMT-FR-ZQKD** A discussion's card holding a **pending question set** renders that set as a block between its comments and its composer, and its composer, its Quote action, and every other route by which the author would add a comment to it are disabled while the set stands, on the terms `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` sets (DQA-FR-NRZB, DQA-FR-PXNC, DQA-FR-VJHT). Reading, resolving, and reopening are untouched.
51. **CMT-FR-56** A resolved whole-target discussion leaves the chooser's tabs for the resolved disclosure and returns to them when it is reopened, on the terms CMT-FR-17 sets for any discussion. An unresolved one keeps its tab like any other (CMT-FR-29), because a conversation waiting on the author is waiting on them whether it names a passage or not.
52. **CMT-FR-61** The column renders the whole-target discussions wherever it renders — in an Editor tab and in a New Artifact tab alike (CMT-FR-37) — because a conversation about the open thing is read in the same place as the remarks about its passages. The column is not the only place a whole-target discussion is read: a tab that renders no column reads its discussions in a floating panel instead (per `ACT-action-control.md` ACT-FR-20), which renders the same shared surface this spec uses and introduces no rendering of its own. The column begins a whole-target discussion through its opening composer, which the tab's **Discuss** focuses (per `ACT-action-control.md` ACT-FR-BRZK), and a fragment-targeted discussion through the Comment affordance (CMT-FR-05).
56. **CMT-FR-66** When an edit replaces the buffer with the WYSIWYG surface's serialisation of it (per `EDT-editor.md` EDT-FR-66, EDT-FR-67) and that serialisation settles a different spelling on some of the Markdown, every fragment is re-resolved against the new buffer as part of that same step, on the terms CMT-FR-18 resolves a fragment on load. A discussion whose passage survives the change in a different spelling keeps its fragment and carries the new range and quote, so no discussion is orphaned by a rewriting the author did not perform; a discussion whose passage the author's own edit destroyed orphans exactly as CMT-FR-20 has it. Re-resolution is not itself an edit — it marks the artifact dirty no more than the edit already did and occupies no position in the undo history (per `EDT-editor.md` EDT-FR-23) — and the fragments it settles reach the backend on the next save through CMT-FR-21.
57. **CMT-FR-67** A comment carrying a reference attachment of either kind is an ordinary comment of its discussion in every other respect (CMT-FR-08, CMT-FR-10, CMT-FR-12, CMT-FR-14): it stacks in order, names its agent author, is quotable, and is immutable, and the control it renders (CMT-FR-48) changes nothing about the conversation — activating it opens a modal and posts nothing. The control renders whatever the proposal has since become, because the proposal's pending, accepted, or rejected state lives in the module that owns it rather than in the attachment (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-60, CMS-FR-66); the column reads that state from `"list draft change proposals"` for a `proposal` and from `"list prompt change proposals"` for a `prompt_proposal` when the tab opens, and follows `"draft change proposals changed"` and `"prompt change proposals changed"` respectively thereafter, on the same terms it follows every other event it redraws from (CMT-FR-04). What a control says while that reading does not hold its proposal is `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-UKIG's, and the reading such a control asks for is CTA-FR-XVNC's.
59. **CMT-FR-70** Every composer the column carries posts on **Ctrl+Enter** on Windows and Linux and **Cmd+Enter** on macOS with the composer focused, on exactly the terms `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` CVP-FR-40 sets — a non-whitespace body only, the newline prevented, nothing at all while the composer is empty, posting, disabled for want of an identity (CMT-FR-24), or mid-IME composition, and the mention picker's own key handling untouched (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-VQFJ). A refusal leaves the body and the pending strip intact and renders its typed error inline exactly as a refusal through the Post control does (CMT-FR-34, CMT-FR-51).
60. **CMT-FR-79** A posted comment renders the tags it was written with exactly as they were stored, routing having rewritten nothing (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-KREF), and the turns it dispatched render **one per agent**: each agent's own pending contribution while its turn runs (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-ZOLW), its own comment when that turn delivers (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-QTNB), and its own failed contribution where it failed recoverably (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-MGVJ). The outcomes are independent — one agent's failure neither suppresses another's answer nor produces a second turn for it (per `../core/AGC-agent-conversations.md` AGC-FR-04) — and **Retry** starts one turn for the one agent whose contribution carries it (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-QDDG), so an author watching a message they sent to three agents sees three answers arrive, or two answers and one offer to retry, and never one agent answering twice.
61. **CMT-FR-83** Every attachment affordance is **operable without a pointer**. In a composer, the **Attach** control, each entry of its menu, and each entry of the pending strip together with that entry's remove control are all reachable and activatable from the keyboard and are named for assistive technology by what they hold (CMT-FR-45, CMT-FR-46). On a posted comment, an image thumbnail is focusable, carries the attachment's own name as its accessible name, and opens at full size from the keyboard exactly as it opens by pointer, and a chip and a could-not-be-loaded state each read as what they are (CMT-FR-48, CMT-FR-49). This holds in every conversation the column renders and is not conditioned on what the conversation is about, so a draft's discussion attaches, removes, focuses, and inspects an image on exactly the terms an artifact's review does (CMT-FR-37, CMT-FR-55) — the affordance is one affordance, and a picture pasted into a draft conversation is attached, rendered, and opened by the same controls everywhere.
62. **CMT-FR-AWIE** A comment body renders with every provider citation marker hidden, by the rule of `../ai/CVL-conversation-loop.md` CVL-FR-VSSU. The stored body is unchanged by it.
71. **CMT-FR-HQNV** Every discussion the column renders — fragment-targeted, orphaned, whole-target, and resolved alike — is the shared discussion surface of `CVP-conversation-presentation.md` (CVP-FR-SDMQ) in its column form, with its composer the shared composer. In this spec a **card** is one discussion as that surface renders it, in the column or in a floating panel. The column adds only what depends on standing beside the text: the highlight of a fragment in the body (CMT-FR-28), the chooser, the unavailable state, and the resolved disclosure. There is no separate comment-thread model: a card renders a discussion, and no card carries a control that moves its discussion into another presentation mode.
72. **CMT-FR-WZTE** A card whose discussion has a fragment target carries the text badge **Fragment**. A card whose discussion has no fragment target carries no target badge: it has no **Fragment** badge and no fragment quote, and that tells the author that it is about the whole owner. The badge is a word and never a colour alone, so a discussion about the whole and a discussion about a line are told apart by reading.
73. **CMT-FR-RPLC** A discussion whose fragment cannot be restored — its quote is found nowhere (CMT-FR-18), or its owner file is unavailable — renders in the orphaned or unavailable state (CMT-FR-19) and is kept. It stays readable, answerable while it is not resolved, and listed in the Comments panel. It is never discarded and never converted to a whole-target discussion.

## Non-functional requirements
- A proposal control costs the column one reading per target — one per draft, one per artifact — rather than one per control, and a control whose proposal has resolved asks for nothing further: the healing ask of `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-LOOE is bounded per reference and never a poll.
- Fragment tracking keeps pace with typing on a document of several thousand lines: the fragment highlights update without perceptible input lag in the editing surface.
- The re-anchor search runs once per discussion when an artifact loads, not per keystroke, so a heavily-commented artifact opens no more slowly than an uncommented one to a noticeable degree.
- Comment bodies are short-form Markdown; a card truncates a very long comment to a readable height and reveals the rest in place rather than widening the column's content column.
- The column covers no part of the page at any window size the shell supports. Showing or hiding it reflows nothing in the file's text and never moves the caret.
- Relative timestamps refresh as time passes without the author reopening the artifact.
- The column holds only what the open artifact's discussions need; an artifact with no discussions costs one list call and no rendering.
- Every column action is reachable by keyboard alone: the Comment affordance, each card, its overflow menu, its composer, and the mention picker within it are focusable and operable without a pointer.
- Posting a comment never waits on an agent: the comment appears in its card as soon as it is appended, and the turns it dispatched resolve into the card afterwards at whatever pace the models answer.
- Deriving a conversation's active agents is a read of the comments the column already holds against the enrolled agents it already holds, and a read that stops at the first comment naming anybody rather than walking the whole discussion, so a follow-up costs no call beyond the turns it dispatches however many agents an `@all` turns out to name, the recipients a composer names in its placeholder cost nothing to keep current, and a conversation nobody has addressed an agent in dispatches nothing at all.
- A draft's discussions and the discussions fragmented in its prompt cost one list call when its tab opens, and nothing afterwards until a discussion is opened or answered.
- A card holding several outstanding turns stays legible: pending contributions occupy the same narrow measure a comment does and never widen the column's content column, and a failed contribution and its Retry occupy that same measure.
- An activity status costs the column no call of its own: it is read from the turn records the column already holds and the events it already follows, so a card says what an agent is doing without asking anything further and a turn that reaches for a dozen tools costs the same reads as one that reaches for none.
- A transient failure the application recovered from costs the author nothing to notice: the card reads **Thinking…** throughout and the answer arrives a moment later, so the only difference between a turn that succeeded first time and one that succeeded on its third attempt is how long the author waited.
- Recovering an offer to retry, and recovering an unsupported-image notice, cost one call each: the column reads `"list recoverable agent turn failures"` and `"list agent turn image notices"` alongside the reads it already performs when an artifact opens, and follows events for every change afterwards, so a conversation carrying no failure costs nothing and one carrying a failure costs no polling.
- The column never asks the backend what it has already been told: a conversation nobody is writing to costs no call after the artifact's first read, and a conversation being written to costs none at all beyond the events it receives.
- An attachment's content is loaded lazily and at the size the card shows it, so a review carrying dozens of screenshots opens as quickly as one carrying none and scrolling the column is not gated on decoding pictures.
- Attaching is bounded by what the backend accepts: the composer states the refusal it received rather than pre-empting it, and a refused attachment never costs the author the comment they had written.
