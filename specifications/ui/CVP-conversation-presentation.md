# Conversation presentation

**Spec code:** `CVP`

## Intent
Decides **how** a discussion is rendered and **where** it is revealed, independently of what it is about. Every conversation of the application — a remark pinned to a passage, a discussion about a whole file, a Flow, a Diff, a History view, a draft, or a note — is one discussion record (`../core/CMS-comments-storage.md`) and is rendered by one shared **discussion surface** with one shared **composer**, so a conversation reads and feels the same wherever the author meets it. The surface sits inside the layout of its owner: the Editor's comment rail, the draft's discussion column, the action control's discussion panel, the Flow panel, the Diff panel, the History panel, or a **conversation tab**. A discussion has **one active owner surface** at a time and never two. A single route, `revealDiscussion`, resolves a discussion id and either focuses the surface that already shows it, or opens the correct owner surface, or — for a note, or for an owner that no longer resolves — opens the conversation tab. No control anywhere moves a discussion between presentation modes, because there are no modes: where a discussion appears is decided by its owner and never by a layout choice the author must manage. What the author has typed, attached, scrolled to, and not yet read stays with the discussion when its owner surface changes, because a surface the author has to rebuild is one they will not use. Out of scope: what a conversation **is** and what it costs to write to one, which stay exactly as `CMT-comments.md` renders fragment-targeted discussions and `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` stores them; the lifecycle a discussion carries, which is the `Active`, `Locked`, and `Resolved` of `CMP-comments-panel.md` CMP-FR-03 and gains no state here; beginning a conversation, which is the owning surface's affordance; persisting any of the presentation state, since it describes where the author is looking rather than anything about the project; and the project-wide Comments panel's own presentation, which stays a read-only vertical-panel index that is neither tabbable nor draggable (per `CMP-comments-panel.md` CMP-FR-16, CMP-FR-23).

## User stories
- As an author working down a review, I want every conversation to look and behave the same, so I learn one surface and use it everywhere.
- As an author who clicks a conversation in the Comments panel, I want to land on that conversation where it belongs, so I never get a second copy of it.
- As an author mid-reply, I want my half-written sentence to survive a change of tab or surface, so an interruption costs me nothing.
- As an author discussing a note, I want a full conversation tab, so I read a conversation rather than a line in a narrow panel.
- As an author whose artifact was deleted, I want the conversation still readable and still answerable, so a file's removal does not silently take a discussion with it.

- Layout notes: the conversation tab renders one column at the tab's own measure, its header pinned above the message list and its composer pinned below it, both staying put while the messages scroll. A card in a narrow margin shows the same parts as the tab, set at the card's measure.

## UI contract boundary
- **Owned by the UI**: the discussion-surface registry and everything in it — the mapping from a discussion's identity to its one active owner surface, the surface's host (owner or conversation tab), its unread, pending, and failed indicators, its pending-focus request, and its owner availability; the single route `revealDiscussion` and the serialization that makes concurrent reveals join one outcome; the two shared components, `DiscussionSurface` and `DiscussionComposer`, that every owner renders; the conversation tab and its chrome; the module-level session stores that hold composer text, pending attachments, scroll position, unread state, pending agent placeholders, failed contributions, and question-set answers across owner changes; the scroll-follow and unread-indicator rule; the shared keyboard rule of the composer; the resolution of a discussion's owner and the unavailable-owner state; the focus targets and accessible announcements of every reveal; and the teardown performed on a project or worktree change.
- **Delegated to backend (abstract)**: `"read discussion"` — owned by `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md`, which owns the discussion and comment records and the append-only log they persist to. It is the one operation this spec introduces, and it exists because every other read of a conversation is keyed by the owner it belongs to, which a conversation tab whose owner is unavailable or absent no longer has. Every surface follows that module's `"discussion changed"` event and redraws from its payload rather than re-reading (CVP-FR-36). Everything a conversation's surface and composer invoke once rendered — `"add comment to discussion (discussion, body, quotes, attachments)"`, `"set discussion lock"`, `"set discussion resolution"`, `"read comment attachment"`, and the turn operations of `../core/AGC-agent-conversations.md`, `"dispatch agent turn"`, `"cancel agent turn"`, `"list agent turns"`, `"list recoverable agent turn failures"`, and `"retry agent turn"` among them — belongs to `CMT-comments.md` (CMT-FR-11, CMT-FR-15, CMT-FR-16, CTA-FR-EACI, CMT-FR-49, CTA-FR-QDDG, CTA-FR-RHPP) and is invoked identically by every owner, so changing the owner surface changes no operation a conversation issues. `"read discussion question set"` and `"submit discussion question answers"`, with the event `"discussion question set changed"`, belong to `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` on those same terms (CVP-FR-QDBX). No surface opens a discussion on its own account; beginning one is the owning surface's affordance alone (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-05, `ACT-action-control.md` ACT-FR-13, and `NTS-notes.md` NTS-FR-27), and the opening post a note's composer makes — `"get or create note discussion (note, body, attachments)"` — is delegated by that spec rather than by this one, although the composer making it is rendered by the conversation tab this spec holds (CVP-FR-60). Nothing here persists anything: no operation of `../core/PSS-project-settings-storage.md` or `../core/GSS-global-settings-storage.md` is invoked from this spec.

## Functional requirements

1. **CVP-FR-01** A **conversation** is one discussion as `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` records it, identified by that discussion's `id` — opaque, stable, and unique within the project. A discussion with a fragment target and one without are conversations on identical terms, whatever their owner is — an artifact, a draft, a note, a Flow, a Diff, or a History view (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-53) — so every rule below binds every combination.
2. **CVP-FR-02** A discussion has at most one **active owner surface** in the application session: the one place, in one owner layout or in one conversation tab, that renders it. A discussion that no surface renders is **closed**: it still exists, is still listed wherever discussions are listed, and is shown by any route that reveals it (CVP-FR-52).
3. **CVP-FR-04** The session-only registry in `src/state/discussionSurfaces.ts` holds one record per discussion id and is the single authority on what is open. A record holds `discussionId`, `owner`, `hostedIn` (`owner` or `tab`), `unread`, `pending`, `failed`, `pendingFocus`, and `ownerAvailable`. For the not-yet-opened discussion of a note the key is the note id until the opening post creates the discussion (CVP-FR-60). Nothing consults an owner layout's own state to decide whether a discussion is already shown.
4. **CVP-FR-05** No discussion is ever rendered twice. When the owner surface that shows a discussion changes — an Editor tab closes and the conversation tab takes over, an owner becomes unavailable, a tab reopens — the one registry record changes its host and the one surface moves with it. No card, panel, tab, or composer instance of one discussion exists beside another.
5. **CVP-FR-06** Every route that reveals a discussion calls `revealDiscussion`, which resolves the discussion's identity first and then routes on the registry and on the owner: a surface that already shows the discussion is focused; otherwise the correct owner surface is opened and the discussion is shown in it; otherwise — for a note, or for an owner that no longer resolves — the conversation tab is opened or focused. This binds every route without exception: a Comments panel row (per `CMP-comments-panel.md` CMP-FR-10), an owning surface's own affordance, **Discuss** on a note, a notification, and any other navigation entry that names a conversation. The full routing is the table of CVP-FR-54.
6. **CVP-FR-07** Reveals of one discussion are **serialized**: while a reveal is in flight, a further reveal of that discussion joins the one in flight and focuses its outcome rather than starting a second. Two requests arriving together leave exactly one surface, whichever order they arrived in.
7. **CVP-FR-08** A discussion's **owner** is the thing it belongs to, and the owner decides which surface shows it: an artifact owner is shown in the Editor tab of that file — in the comment rail for a fragment-targeted discussion and in the rail's Discussion section or the action control's discussion panel for a whole-target one (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-01, `ACT-action-control.md` ACT-FR-19, ACT-FR-20); a Flow, Diff, or History discussion is shown in the panel of that view; a draft owner is shown in the draft's discussion column (CVP-FR-DKPW); a note owner is shown in a conversation tab and nowhere else (CVP-FR-59).
8. **CVP-FR-SDMQ** Every owner layout renders a conversation with the same two shared components. `DiscussionSurface` renders the message list — Markdown bodies through the shared `CommentMarkdown` rendering, quotes, attachments, agent pending and failed contributions with their **Retry**, the question-set block, the **Lock** and **Resolve** controls, the unread divider and indicator, and the scroll-follow behaviour of CVP-FR-TWRL. `DiscussionComposer` renders the one opening or reply composer: the Markdown input, the agent mention picker, attachments, validation, typed-error and identity-error display, and the keyboard rule of CVP-FR-40. Owners keep their own layouts and place these two components inside them. No owner draws its own message list or composer.
9. **CVP-FR-TIBK** `DiscussionSurface` and `DiscussionComposer` take the author label of every comment from the one rule of `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-ZCAE, on every owner layout and in every conversation tab. The composer is enabled for **Me**. Labels refresh on `"github tokens changed"` without a re-read, a remount, or a scroll change.
9. **CVP-FR-HBKV** No control anywhere moves a discussion between presentation modes. There is no Detach, Maximize, Minimize, or Attach action, no floating conversation window, no bookmark strip, no placeholder standing for a card shown elsewhere, and no overlay geometry. A conversation tab is the only surface a discussion is hosted in outside its owner layout.
10. **CVP-FR-TWRL** A message list **opens at its foot**, because a conversation is opened to see what was said last. While the author is at the foot, a message that arrives keeps the list at the foot (auto-follow). Once the author scrolls away from the foot, the list does not move: a message that arrives marks the discussion unread and shows an **unread indicator** — a labelled control naming the number of new messages, with an unread divider above the first of them — that scrolls to the first unread message when activated. Reaching the foot, or activating the indicator, clears the unread state. A list that moved itself while the author read it would be worse than one that needed a scroll.
11. **CVP-FR-32** Every surface renders the conversation as a **chronological group chat**: human and agent messages stacked oldest first with every sender identified, Markdown bodies rendered as rich text, quotes rendered as attributed blocks, attachments rendered below the body they belong to, pending contributions rendered in the position the answer will take and reading the same activity status there as in any other owner, failed contributions and their **Retry** control rendered in that same position, and typed operation errors rendered inline — each exactly as `CMT-comments.md` defines it (CMT-FR-08, CMT-FR-09, CMT-FR-10, CMT-FR-13, CMT-FR-34, CTA-FR-ZOLW, CMT-FR-48, CTA-FR-MGVJ, CTA-FR-QDDG, CTA-FR-FBJR, CTA-FR-IWOJ). A message reads the same wherever it is read.
    In the conversation tab the stack is arranged as **two columns**. A message the author wrote themselves sits against the **trailing** edge and every message written to them — by a person or by an agent alike — sits against the **leading** edge, each in a bounded block rather than across the full measure. The split says *mine* and *not mine* and nothing about who is on the other end, and it is drawn from the participant the backend stamped (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-10). A message on the trailing side carries its own surface as well as its own side, so the distinction does not rest on position alone (CVP-FR-44). The author's own name is dropped from their own messages. In a card, a panel, or a column the stack keeps a single column, where every message already names its author on its own line.
12. **CVP-FR-33** A conversation is **continued** from the one surface that shows it: that surface's composer posts through `"add comment to discussion (discussion, body, quotes, attachments)"` and dispatches turns on exactly the terms the rail does (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-11, `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-EACI, CTA-FR-QUXJ), so the agents a message reaches do not depend on where it was typed. The composer names who an untagged message will reach in its **placeholder** on those same terms and states it nowhere else (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-IAKP). Every owner derives the conversation's active agents afresh from its own comments and the project's current roster rather than from anything the registry carries, so the answer is the same in every owner and the same again after the owner changes.
13. **CVP-FR-QDBX** A discussion holding a **pending question set** renders that set above its composer in whichever surface shows it, and disables that composer while the set stands, on the terms `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` sets (DQA-FR-NRZB, DQA-FR-PXNC). A change of owner surface carries the block and the unsent answer draft with the discussion like any other session state (CVP-FR-47).
14. **CVP-FR-34** Lock and resolve are offered by every surface on the terms `CMT-comments.md` sets (CMT-FR-15, CMT-FR-16): a locked or resolved discussion carries no composer and no Quote action in any surface. The lifecycle states are `Active`, `Locked`, and `Resolved` and this spec adds none.
15. **CVP-FR-36** Every surface follows the `"discussion changed"` event and redraws from its payload (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-51) and follows `"agent turn state changed"` for its outstanding turns and for the recoverable failures they terminate in (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-ZOLW, CTA-FR-RHPP). A new comment, an agent's answer, a lock, a resolution, and a turn that could not reach its model appear in the active surface immediately. A surface reading a conversation for itself (CVP-FR-53) reads `"list recoverable agent turn failures"` for it alongside `"list agent turns"`, so a surface mounted while a failure stands renders it.
16. **CVP-FR-HWTN** The session store of CVP-FR-47 follows `"agent turn state changed"` for every discussion, also while no surface for that discussion is mounted. A turn that has terminated or was cancelled never renders as pending again, also when a later `"dispatch agent turn"` result reports it as running.
    - *Why:* The dispatch result and the turn's events arrive in no fixed order, so a late result can otherwise bring back a finished turn.
16. **CVP-FR-37** A registry record carries **unread**, **pending-response**, and **failed-response** indicators, all session state: a message arriving in a discussion whose surface the author is not currently looking at marks it unread, focusing that surface and reaching its foot clears it, an outstanding turn marks it pending, and a conversation carrying a failed contribution awaiting Retry (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-RHPP) marks it failed. A conversation retrying a model call automatically is pending and not failed; a turn that failed recoverably clears the pending mark and sets the failed one; and taking the Retry, or a later human comment retiring the offer, clears it again. Conversation tabs and Comments panel rows render these indicators from the same record.
17. **CVP-FR-40** With a composer focused in any surface, **Ctrl+Enter** on Windows and Linux and **Cmd+Enter** on macOS invokes the existing Post operation and prevents the newline the key would otherwise insert. It acts only on a body that is not empty and not whitespace alone, and does nothing at all while the composer is empty, while a post is already in flight, while the composer is disabled for want of a resolved author identity (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-24), while the discussion is locked, and while an IME composition is in progress. It leaves the mention picker's own key handling untouched, so Enter completes a nickname where the picker is open and the accelerator posts where it is not.
18. **CVP-FR-41** A post the backend refuses through the accelerator behaves exactly as one refused through the Post control: the typed error renders inline, and the body and every pending attachment are retained (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-34, CMT-FR-51).
19. **CVP-FR-42** Every reveal has a defined focus target. A reveal that focuses an existing surface, or opens an owner surface or the conversation tab, lands focus on the discussion in that surface, and on its composer where the composer held focus when the surface last lost it, with its unsent text intact. Where the owner surface mounts later than the route runs, the registry's `pendingFocus` request is held and consumed by the surface when it mounts, so the focus is never lost to timing.
20. **CVP-FR-43** Every reveal is **announced** accessibly, naming the discussion and the surface it landed on. The arrival of a new message, the start and the end of a pending response, an operation failure, a reveal that focused an existing surface rather than opening one, and an owner becoming unavailable are each announced on the same terms.
21. **CVP-FR-44** Every control this spec introduces — the unread indicator, the conversation tab's controls, and the composer's controls — is reachable and operable by keyboard alone and carries the name it would have spelled out. Every state it introduces — unread, pending, failed, fragment or whole, locked, resolved, unavailable owner — is carried by more than colour, so none of it depends on distinguishing two hues. Each such state is a word, a glyph with an accessible name, or both.
22. **CVP-FR-45** An owner is **unavailable** when the thing the discussion is about no longer resolves in the active project — the artifact deleted, the file moved where the surface could not follow it — while the project and worktree the registry belongs to are still the active ones. The conversation stays discoverable by its stable identity and its surface renders an explicit unavailable-owner state naming what is missing.
23. **CVP-FR-46** An unavailable owner disables only what needs the owner: there is no owner surface to open, so `revealDiscussion` opens the fallback conversation tab (CVP-FR-54). Reading the conversation, scrolling it, quoting it, locking it, resolving it, and posting to it are all untouched, and the composer stays enabled: posting continues through the existing storage and worktree rules and retains its body and pending attachments on a typed backend refusal, because a log whose file has gone is still a log this module writes to (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-25).
24. **CVP-FR-47** No owner change and no ordinary navigation loses a discussion's **session state**: its unsent composer text, its pending attachments, its scroll position, its unread state, its pending agent placeholders and outstanding turns, its failed contributions and the Retry they offer, its unsupported-image notice (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-ARBB), its inline failures, and its question-set answers. They are held in module-level session stores keyed by discussion id — or by the discussion target key for an opening composer that has no discussion id yet — so they survive switching tabs, switching panel surfaces, closing and reopening the owner tab, and a change of host between an owner layout and the conversation tab.
25. **CVP-FR-48** None of it is persisted. The registry, every surface, the unread, pending, and failed indicators, failed contributions and their offers to retry, unsupported-image notices, scroll positions, and unsent composer text and pending attachments are held in memory alone, so an application restart finds no surface of any kind: no conversation tab and no unsent text. What survives a restart is the conversation itself — its comments, its lifecycle, its attachments, and any **pending question set** a discussion holds, each on its own persistence contract (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-04, CMS-FR-XWDA) — and a pending or failed agent turn after a restart is whatever `../core/AGC-agent-conversations.md` reports it to be (AGC-FR-23).
    The session-only rule binds **presentation state** and binds nothing else. A pending question set is a durable record of the conversation, this spec neither holds nor discards it, and a fresh surface opened after a restart renders the set it finds (per `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` DQA-FR-JWEF). What stays session memory there is the author's **unsent answer draft**, which is gone with the session (DQA-FR-ZUPB).
26. **CVP-FR-49** Changing the active project (per `OVW-overview.md` OVW-FR-11) or the active worktree (per `OVW-overview.md` OVW-FR-12) **tears down every surface**, exactly as application exit does: every conversation tab, every registry record, and every session store go, and unsent composer text and pending attachments are discarded with them on the same terms every other session-memory state is discarded by that switch. Nothing is retained as an unavailable-owner record across the switch.
27. **CVP-FR-50** That teardown changes **nothing** about any conversation: no discussion is resolved, locked, deleted, retargeted, or written to by it, and no operation of `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` is invoked. Opening the outgoing worktree again finds every conversation as it was.
28. **CVP-FR-51** **Deleting** a draft deletes that draft's conversations outright (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-39), so every surface and registry record of one is torn down with it. This is not the unavailable-owner state of CVP-FR-45: that state keeps a conversation that still exists reachable, and there is nothing here left to reach. **Graduating** a draft tears nothing down: a graduated draft keeps its discussions and its whole folder (per `../core/DRS-draft-storage.md` DRS-FR-20 and `NAW-new-artifact.md` NAW-FR-20), so every surface of one of its conversations stays open, keeps its history and its scroll position, and goes on rendering. What the conversation refuses afterwards is a **contribution**, on the ordinary terms a locked or a settled conversation refuses one.
29. **CVP-FR-52** Revealing a closed discussion creates exactly **one** fresh registry record, initialized from the discussion as `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` currently holds it and from the session-memory rules above — with no unread state and no remembered scroll position unless a session store still holds them. The host it opens in is the one `revealDiscussion` selects: the owner surface for an available artifact or draft owner, and the conversation tab for a note or an unavailable owner (CVP-FR-54).
30. **CVP-FR-53** A discussion is read from `"read discussion"` when the surface showing it has no owner layout to read it through — a conversation tab opened for a note, a conversation tab whose owner is unavailable (CVP-FR-45), and a conversation tab whose owning tab is closed. A surface whose owner layout is mounted takes the discussion from the list that layout already performed and issues no read of its own.
31. **CVP-FR-54** `revealDiscussion` routes exactly as this table states. In every row the discussion's records and the session state of CVP-FR-47 are preserved unchanged, no second surface is created, and no mode-transition control is involved.

    | Discussion state | Owner | Route | Surface that shows it | Focus lands on |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | Shown in an owner layout already | Available artifact or draft | Focus | The existing owner surface; its Editor, New Artifact, Flow, Diff, or History tab is selected | The discussion in that surface |
    | Shown in a conversation tab already | Any | Focus | The existing conversation tab | The discussion in the tab |
    | Closed | Available artifact | Open | The Editor tab of the artifact, on the surface that renders the rail; the fragment is shown where the discussion has one | The discussion in the rail, or in the Discussion section or panel |
    | Closed | Available draft | Open | The New Artifact tab of the draft; its discussion column or its comment rail | The discussion in that surface |
    | Closed, from a Flow, Diff, or History origin | Available artifact | Open | The panel of that origin in the artifact's tab | The discussion in that panel |
    | Closed or shown | Note | Conversation tab | One conversation tab for the note's discussion | The discussion, or the opening composer where the note has none |
    | Closed or shown | Unavailable owner | Fallback conversation tab | One conversation tab whose header names the unavailable owner | The discussion in the tab |
    | None, by **Discuss** on a note with no discussion | Note | Conversation tab, opening state | One conversation tab holding the opening composer | The opening composer |
    | Opening state, by the opening post succeeding | Note | Same tab | The same tab, now showing the created discussion | The reply composer |
    | Opening state, by cancel or close | Note | Closed | None | The Notes panel row |
    | Any, by the owner becoming unavailable | Unavailable owner | Host change | The conversation tab, when the surface was in the owner layout | The surface the author was on |
    | Any, by the note being deleted | Note | Torn down | None | The Notes panel |

32. **CVP-FR-55** A composer is **one field** carrying the message and the two controls that act on it: the **Attach** affordance leading it and the **Post** action trailing it, both on the message's own line rather than in a row of their own beneath it, so writing a reply and sending it are one gesture in one place. The pending-attachment strip sits inside that field below the line the message is written on. Focus lights the whole field rather than a box inside it. It is the same field in the rail's card, in every owner layout, and in the conversation tab. The surface the Attach affordance opens hangs from the edge of the control that has the field behind it and opens across the conversation, never outward past the edge of its container — a surface the surrounding container clips is a surface whose entries cannot be reached at all.
    The composer additionally carries the control that discards what has been written, rendered only while there is something to discard — except on the draft card that opens a new fragment-targeted discussion, where it is unconditional, because there it abandons the draft itself rather than its contents and an empty draft still has to be dismissible (per `CMT-comments.md` CMT-FR-07).
33. **CVP-FR-56** Every composer opens at the height of a **single line** and grows with what is typed up to **five lines**, and no further: past that it scrolls within itself rather than taking the surface from the conversation it is answering. One line because that is the size of most replies and a permanently taller box spends the surface on nothing; a ceiling because a composer that grows without bound pushes the messages it is answering off the surface. Every owner shares the one behaviour.
34. **CVP-FR-57** A discussion hosted in a conversation tab is named **`Chat: <owner>`** — `Chat: onboarding.md` for a conversation on the artifact of that name — and carries that name identically in the tab's header and in the tab's entry in the strip. The tab floats free of the owner layout that would otherwise say what the conversation is about, and the owner's name does. The fragment quote or discussion label remains the conversation's **subject** rather than its name — it follows the name on hover titles, on accessible names, and in spoken announcements.
35. **CVP-FR-59** A discussion owned by a **note** is shown in a conversation tab and in no other surface. The Notes panel is list-only and renders **no** card, no composer, and no conversation of any kind inside a note's row or in any Notes-panel subview — a row in a narrow panel is a line of text with an overflow menu, not a surface a conversation is read in. **Discuss** on a note calls `revealDiscussion` (per `NTS-notes.md` NTS-FR-27). Closing the conversation tab removes only the session surface: it deletes no persisted record and changes no lifecycle state, so the conversation stands exactly as it stood and **Discuss** on the note reopens it with every message in it.
36. **CVP-FR-60** A note's not-yet-opened discussion is keyed in the registry by its **note's** stable id, which lets the surface exist before the discussion does. Choosing **Discuss** on a note with no discussion opens exactly one conversation tab in an **opening state**: it renders the shared composer alone — no message list, no lifecycle state, and nothing read from the backend — and creating the discussion is the author's post and nothing else, so mounting it invokes no operation, creates no discussion, writes no association, and dispatches no agent turn. A second **Discuss** on that note joins the reveal in flight and focuses the tab (CVP-FR-07). Posting the opening message turns that same tab into an ordinary tab for the discussion the backend returned, in place and without a remount, with the opening message its first rendered comment. Closing or cancelling before the post removes the tab and the unsent text and pending attachments with it, leaving nothing persisted anywhere. Once the discussion exists, the record is keyed by its discussion id, which is the same conversation on every route and after every restart.
37. **CVP-FR-61** Deleting a note tears down its conversation tab outright, because the conversation itself is deleted with the note (per `../core/NTC-notes-storage.md` NTC-FR-21). This is not the unavailable-owner state of CVP-FR-45, on the same terms a deleted draft's conversations are not (CVP-FR-51). The registry is session memory and never the authority on whether the records went, so the teardown follows the backend's reported success and a failed cleanup tears down nothing, leaving the conversation open and readable while the Notes panel reports the typed error (per `NTS-notes.md` NTS-FR-30). Nothing can reopen a deleted note's conversation afterwards: the row is gone, no index lists it, and a request naming its discussion is refused (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-62).
38. **CVP-FR-62** **Retry** is offered by every surface on identical terms and invokes `"retry agent turn"` from the surface that currently shows the conversation, on exactly the terms every other operation a conversation issues is invoked from there (CVP-FR-33): a turn retried from a rail card, a panel, or a conversation tab reaches the same agent with the same origin and the same trigger comment. The failed contribution is session state and stays with the discussion across owner changes (CVP-FR-47), and taking the Retry dispatches once from the surface it now lives in.
39. **CVP-FR-63** Every surface renders an agent comment's **title** exactly as the rail's card does (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-KFUF): a non-empty snapshot on a second line directly below the agent's name, and no line and no placeholder where the participant carries none or an empty one. It comes from that comment's own participant and from nowhere else, so a conversation tab reading a discussion for itself (CVP-FR-53) renders the same titles as one whose owner is open.
40. **CVP-FR-64** A discussion created by the opening composer of `ACT-action-control.md` ACT-FR-16 — never a note's, whose own opening composer is CVP-FR-60's — is registered the moment `"open discussion (target, fragment target, body, attachments)"` returns it, with one fresh registry record hosted in its owner surface, initialized with no unread state and no failed contribution. Its owning surface — the rail's Discussion section or the action control's discussion panel, whichever of `ACT-action-control.md` ACT-FR-19 and ACT-FR-20 applies — shows it as an ordinary conversation from the instant it is first listed there, and focus lands in its reply composer (CVP-FR-42). Two activation or post events racing on the same not-yet-created discussion resolve to this outcome exactly once, on the same serialization terms as any reveal (CVP-FR-07): `ACT-action-control.md` ACT-FR-16 invokes the open operation once regardless, so there is never a second record for this registry to reconcile.
41. **CVP-FR-DKPW** A discussion owned by a **draft** is shown in its draft's own discussion column (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-NWRL), which renders the shared `DiscussionSurface` and `DiscussionComposer`, and its registry record is hosted in that owner. `revealDiscussion` selects the draft's New Artifact tab and focuses the discussion there (CVP-FR-54). The column is always visible beside the draft, so no host change is needed to read it.

## Non-functional requirements
- An owner change is a re-parenting of a live conversation rather than a remount of it: no change re-reads the conversation, re-decodes an attachment, or restarts an outstanding agent turn, and none costs a visible flash of an empty surface.
- Every surface of one discussion reads the same events. A conversation nobody is writing to costs nothing after the read that first rendered it, and one being written to costs the events it receives and no call at all.
- The registry costs nothing for a discussion nobody has revealed: a project whose author has never opened a conversation tab holds records for the surfaces that are mounted and behaves in every respect as it would without a registry.
- Everything here stays usable and legible at the minimum window size the shell supports (≥1280×800).
- The registry holds nothing for `ACT-action-control.md`'s opening composer while it is unposted: composing, attaching, and dismissing it before a successful post touches no record and costs this module no call (CVP-FR-64).
- Session stores for composer text, attachments, scroll position, and question-set answers hold only what the author has not yet sent, so a long session costs one small record per discussion the author touched.
