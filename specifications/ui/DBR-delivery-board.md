# Delivery board

**Spec code:** `DBR`

## Intent
The delivery board is the layout of the canvas in the Delivery lens of the Map tab (`MDL-map-delivery-lens.md`). It shows the project's delivery the way a team reads a kanban board: each milestone is a column, the epics and features in it are groups, and each task and defect is a compact card of three lines: what it is, its name, and where it stands and who has it. The board puts every card in an exact slot of a stack, so cards line up and the plan reads at a glance. The board follows the Synthesis design system: quiet surfaces, 1px borders, a kind chip and a status dot in place of glyphs, and colour only where it carries a meaning. The architect plans by moving cards: a drag takes a card or a group from one stack to another stack, in the same milestone or in another, and the stacks open and close around it as it moves, so the architect sees where it lands before the drop. A drop that changes the container is an edit of the plan, which for a GitHub plan goes to GitHub at once (`MDL-map-delivery-lens.md` MDL-FR-PUCM). The order of the cards in one stack is the architect's own arrangement, and it is kept on that machine with the rest of the layout (`../core/DMS-delivery-machine-state.md`). The board applies to a delivery from a GitHub Project and to a delivery from a Flow file. Out of scope: a Flow file that is not the delivery, which keeps the free canvas of `FLO-flow.md` in its Flow tab; swimlanes across milestones; and moving an item with the keyboard.

## Functional requirements
1. **DBR-FR-ERHW** In the Delivery lens, the canvas of `FLO-flow.md` lays the plan out as a **board** for a `github` source and for a `file` source. A Flow tab never shows the board.
2. **DBR-FR-ZGNB** Each top-level milestone is a **column**, and the last column is **No milestone** (per `FLO-flow.md` FLO-FR-SQUS, FLO-FR-IAOQ, and FLO-FR-MCFR). All columns have one width and their tops align. The width is the width that the deepest nesting of groups in the plan needs around a card of the board width (DBR-FR-LNZI).
3. **DBR-FR-IQBN** An epic and a feature are **groups**. A group renders as a header line and, below it, the **stack** of its members. A column also holds a stack. A group fills the inner width of the stack that holds it, and a card fills the inner width of its stack, so every edge of a stack is straight.
4. **DBR-FR-FISH** A stack puts its members one below the other, with one fixed gap between two members, groups and cards mixed in the stack order (DBR-FR-HTCE). No member overlaps another member, and no member has a free position.
5. **DBR-FR-LNZI** A task and a defect are **cards**. A card has three lines: the top line, the name line, and the bottom line. Its height is the height of these lines, and the name line takes a second line only where the name wraps (DBR-FR-LVBY). The error strip of DBR-FR-CJSG adds its height. Nothing else changes the height of a card.
6. **DBR-FR-XOOU** The **top line** of a card shows the kind chip (DBR-FR-DZSU) and, for an item of a GitHub plan, the **address** `#number` of its issue in the monospace face. The tooltip and the accessible name of the address hold the full address `owner/name#number`. The address is the tracker tag of `FLO-flow.md` FLO-FR-ULEM: activating it invokes `"open tracker link (url)"` with the issue's URL.
7. **DBR-FR-DZSU** The **kind chip** is a three-letter code in the monospace face, in the chip colours of its kind: `EPC` for an epic, `FTR` for a feature, `TSK` for a task, and `DEF` for a defect. Its accessible name is the kind name.
8. **DBR-FR-LVBY** The **name line** of a card shows the item's name on at most two lines. A name that does not fit in two lines ends in an ellipsis, and the card's tooltip shows the full name.
9. **DBR-FR-HWHO** The **bottom line** of a card shows the item's status in lowercase. For an item of a GitHub plan, it is the issue's `Status` name (per `../core/GDP-github-delivery-plan.md` GDP-FR-QGFO), and an issue with no `Status` shows `no status`. For an item of a Flow file, it is the name of its delivery state (per `FLO-flow.md` FLO-FR-KQCE).
10. **DBR-FR-QZPA** The bottom line starts with the **status dot**: a 6px dot that sorts the status into one of five colours. The colours are **backlog** (neutral), **ready** (accent), **in progress** (live, with a slow pulse), **in review** (warn), and **done** (ok). The status word beside the dot is always present. An item with no status shows no dot.
11. **DBR-FR-TCPE** For an item of a GitHub plan, the dot comes from the issue's `Status` name, compared case-insensitively. The resolved Ready status (per `../core/GPP-github-polling.md` GPP-FR-VXSB) and `Ready` give **ready**. The resolved In Progress status and `In progress` give **in progress**. `In review` gives **in review**. `Done`, or a closed issue, gives **done**. Every other name gives **backlog**.
12. **DBR-FR-GHDZ** For an item of a Flow file, the dot comes from its delivery state (FLO-FR-KQCE): **Drafting** gives **ready**, **In progress** gives **in progress**, **Done** gives **done**, and **Not started** and **Dropped** give **backlog**.
13. **DBR-FR-GFGG** The bottom line ends with the item's **markers**, then its assignee (DBR-FR-WQAN). Each marker is a badge with a lowercase word, in this order: `pending` (per `MDL-map-delivery-lens.md` MDL-FR-PUCM), `proposed` or `withdrawn` (DBR-FR-AGIN), `blocked` (per FLO-FR-IKMM), `overlap` (per FLO-FR-KWFH), and `draft` for a draft link. A draft that the delivery state reports as missing shows `draft missing` (per FLO-FR-QZUR).
14. **DBR-FR-WQAN** For an item of a GitHub plan with at least one assignee (per `../core/GDP-github-delivery-plan.md` GDP-FR-QGFO), the bottom line shows the **assignee** at its trailing edge, after the markers: `@login` of the first assignee in the monospace face, and `+N` after it where the issue has N more assignees. The tooltip and the accessible name of the assignee hold every login. An issue with no assignee, a milestone, and an item of a Flow file show no assignee. A login wider than 16 characters ends in an ellipsis. The markers, the `+N` count, and the assignee up to that limit keep their full width, and a status word that does not fit beside them ends in an ellipsis.
15. **DBR-FR-ZBVZ** Each marker keeps its tooltip and its activation. The tooltip of the draft marker names the draft. Activating **Blocked** or **Overlap** selects the items it names. The draft row, the **Unlink** action, and the open action show in the issue view of the item (per `MIV-map-issue-view.md`).
16. **DBR-FR-AGIN** A card takes the **proposal mark** while a row of a plan proposal (per `MPV-map-proposal-review.md` MPV-FR-UXHR) that names its issue is `pending` or `applying`: `proposed` on the accent badge for a `changed` row, and `withdrawn` on the danger badge for a `withdrawn` row. The proposals are those of the active Map thread and the one the review sheet shows.
17. **DBR-FR-WKNI** A `pending` or `applying` `added` row shows a **ghost card** in the column of its milestone, or in the **No milestone** column: a dashed border in the ok tone, the kind chip, `new`, the title, and the `added` badge on the ok tone. A ghost card cannot be dragged, renamed, or opened, and opens no issue view.
18. **DBR-FR-PMGS** While the review sheet shows, the card of its selected row takes the selected ring of a card, the ghost card included. An applied row and a rejected row take away their mark and their ghost card, and the next answer of the plan shows the issue an applied row wrote.
19. **DBR-FR-ZRBD** A card shows no reference, no excerpt, and no draft row. The issue view of the item shows all of them (per `MIV-map-issue-view.md` MIV-FR-XMMS and MIV-FR-VHDP).
20. **DBR-FR-CJSG** An error that a requirement shows on an item (per `MDL-map-delivery-lens.md` MDL-FR-PUCM and MDL-FR-HNLP, and FLO-FR-NRBV) gives its card or its group the error border and an **error strip** below its bottom line. The strip holds the error text on one line, and its tooltip holds the full text. The issue view of the item shows the error text below its title.
21. **DBR-FR-SBTR** A group has the face of a card: the top line, the name line, and the bottom line of DBR-FR-XOOU, DBR-FR-LVBY, and DBR-FR-HWHO, with its status dot and its markers. A group that holds items also shows, at the trailing edge of its top line, the count `done/total` of the leaves under it, and the collapse toggle.
22. **DBR-FR-SBLK** The stack of a group sits below its face, behind a divider, on the panel ground. A group that holds no item shows no stack, no placeholder text, no count, and no collapse toggle. A collapsed group shows its face alone, and its count stands in place of the counts line of FLO-FR-ZASL. The frame of a collapsed group, and of a group that holds no item, is as high as its face, which is the height of its slot in its stack (DBR-FR-FISH): the fixed collapsed height of the Flow tab does not apply on the board.
23. **DBR-FR-BYQX** A column header shows the collapse toggle, the milestone flag, the name, the milestone's state as a lowercase badge (FLO-FR-KQCE), the due date (FLO-FR-XAPK), and, at the trailing edge, the count `done/total` of the leaves in the column in the monospace face. The milestone's description shows below this row as one muted paragraph of at most two lines. Dragging a column header changes the milestone order (per FLO-FR-OGPF).
24. **DBR-FR-VNSJ** The count `done/total` of a group or a column counts the leaves under it, and a leaf is done where its status dot is **done** (DBR-FR-QZPA). In a GitHub plan, the issue view of a milestone shows its tracker tag (FLO-FR-ULEM).
25. **DBR-FR-HTCE** The **stack order** of a stack is the order of the stored positions of its members in the layout of the lens's scope (per `../core/DMS-delivery-machine-state.md` DMS-FR-DMWD), from the smallest vertical position to the largest. The members with no stored position follow them, in the automatic order of `FLO-flow.md` FLO-FR-PLUZ: groups before cards, a member after each member it waits for, and plan order for the rest. A new item thus joins the end of its stack.
26. **DBR-FR-MYVI** A press and a move of more than 4 pixels on a card or on a group header starts a **drag** of that item. A press with no such move is a click (per `MIV-map-issue-view.md` MIV-FR-SVXH). A drag of a group takes the group with every item in it. A drag never starts from the address, a marker glyph, the collapse toggle, or the connection handle of FLO-FR-32.
27. **DBR-FR-QCAJ** While a drag runs, the item follows the pointer, raised above the board with a shadow, at the size it has in its stack. Its slot in the stack it left closes, and the members below it move up to fill the gap.
28. **DBR-FR-CZEU** While a drag runs, the **target stack** is the innermost stack under the pointer whose holder can hold the item's kind (per `../core/FGV-flow-graph-validation.md` FGV-FR-YHFX): a group, a milestone column, or the No milestone column. A collapsed group, and a group that holds no item, under the pointer is a target that takes the item at the end of its stack. A group that holds no item shows its stack while it is the target. The target shows an insertion **placeholder** of the dragged item's height at the slot where the item would land: before the first member whose vertical middle is below the pointer, else at the end. The members after the placeholder move down to make room. The target stack and its holder take the drop-target emphasis.
29. **DBR-FR-DQRX** Each move of the members of a stack, as a slot closes, a placeholder opens, or a placeholder moves, is animated over a short time, so a member slides to its new slot and never jumps. With the system setting for reduced motion, the members take their new slots with no animation.
30. **DBR-FR-EEKD** A stack under the pointer whose holder cannot hold the item's kind shows no placeholder. The pointer shows that a drop is not allowed, and a hint near the pointer states which containers the kind can join (per FLO-FR-OAOZ).
31. **DBR-FR-IQOM** While a drag runs near an edge of the canvas, the view pans toward that edge, faster as the pointer comes nearer the edge, so the item reaches a column or a group that is out of the view.
32. **DBR-FR-KTJS** A release over a placeholder **drops** the item into that slot. The item takes the slot at once, and the board writes the layout of the lens's scope once, through `"save delivery layout (scope, layout)"`, with the slot position of each member of the stacks that the drop changed. A drop that changes the container of the item is also an edit (per `FLO-flow.md` FLO-FR-26 and FLO-FR-42): the `move` edit through `"apply github delivery edit (edit)"` for a GitHub plan (per `MDL-map-delivery-lens.md` MDL-FR-XJBV and MDL-FR-PUCM), and a change of the item's container in the document for a Flow file. A drop in the same stack is the layout alone (per FLO-FR-TDHR).
33. **DBR-FR-BBDZ** A drop that FLO-FR-GZCH or FLO-FR-OAOZ refuses, a release where no placeholder shows, a release outside the canvas, and **Escape** during the drag put the item back in its slot with the animation of DBR-FR-DQRX. They change no plan and no layout. A refused drop gives the feedback of the requirement that refused it.
34. **DBR-FR-CVJY** A drop of a GitHub plan that the backend refuses (per MDL-FR-PUCM) puts the item back in the stack and the slot that the last answer gives it, with the animation of DBR-FR-DQRX, and the item shows the error of DBR-FR-CJSG.
35. **DBR-FR-BKWE** On the board, an item has no free position and a group has no stored size. An item moves only by a drop into a slot, and the board offers no resize of a group: FLO-FR-21, FLO-FR-HBBF, and FLO-FR-44 do not apply, and the stored positions are read as the stack order alone (DBR-FR-HTCE). **Reset layout** (per `MDL-map-delivery-lens.md` MDL-FR-QAJN) gives every stack its automatic order and expands every group.
36. **DBR-FR-CCAS** The edges of the plan render between the cards and the groups of the board on the terms of `FLO-flow.md` FLO-FR-39 and FLO-FR-48, and a connection is drawn on the terms of FLO-FR-32 and FLO-FR-33. While a drag runs, the edges of the dragged item follow it.
36. **DBR-FR-SKSO** Where the host hides items (per `MDL-map-delivery-lens.md` MDL-FR-MIMF), a hidden item takes no slot, and its stack closes up. A group whose members are all hidden shows its face alone. The count `done/total` (DBR-FR-VNSJ) still counts every leaf.
36. **DBR-FR-NIGZ** A drop into a stack that holds hidden members lands before the visible member at the slot, or after the last member of the stack where no visible member follows. Each hidden item keeps its place in the order of its stack.
36. **DBR-FR-POOT** An edge with a hidden end is not drawn. A hidden blocker still appears in the tooltip of the **Blocked** marker of the item that it blocks.

## User stories
- As an architect, I want every card on the board to line up in its stack, so that I read the plan as a board and not as a pile of boxes.
- As an architect, I want each card to show its kind, its issue number, its name, its status, and its assignee on three short lines, so that I find any issue at a glance and know where it stands and who works on it.
- As an architect, I want to drag a task to another milestone or epic and see the stack open where it will land, so that I re-plan in one gesture and trust where the task goes.
- As an architect, I want the order I give the cards in a stack to stay when I open the Map again, so that my priorities read top to bottom.

- Layout notes: a column is 304px wide on the panel ground with a 1px border and the medium radius. A card and a group face sit on the canvas ground with a 1px border, the medium radius, and no shadow. A hovered card takes the strongest border. A selected card, group, or column takes the accent border and the 3px focus ring. A dragged item takes the raised shadow, the only shadow of a card. The kind chip, the address, the counts, the due date, and the assignee use the monospace face. The assignee has the muted text colour. The markers are the badges of the design system: `pending` live, `blocked` danger, `overlap` and `draft missing` warn, and `draft` neutral. The error strip is the danger text on the danger-soft ground. The link between two columns is a 1px line with a small arrowhead at the height of the column's first header row. The placeholder has a dashed border and the height of the dragged item. A drop-target stack takes the accent tint of its holder's border. The canvas keeps its 16px dot grid. Every colour, radius, shadow, and motion comes from the design-system tokens in `src/styles/colors_and_type.css`, in both themes.

## UI contract boundary
- **Owned by the UI**: the board layout, the column width, the stacks and their order, the card, the group face, the column header, the drag with its placeholder, its animation, its auto-pan, its refusal, and its return, and the write of the stack order into the layout.
- **Delegated to backend (abstract)**:
  - `"save delivery layout (scope, layout)"` and `"load delivery layout (scope)"` — owned by `../core/DMS-delivery-machine-state.md`.
  - `"apply github delivery edit (edit)"` and `"open tracker link (url)"` — owned by `../core/GDP-github-delivery-plan.md`.

  The canvas, its edges, its selection panel, and its plan rules are owned by `FLO-flow.md`. The lens, its sources, and its pending edits are owned by `MDL-map-delivery-lens.md`.

## Non-functional requirements
- A drag follows the pointer at the frame rate of the display on a plan of several hundred items. A drag reads no backend and writes no layout until the drop.
- A drop writes the layout once and sends at most one edit.
- The board shows no item at a size or a place other than its slot, so a screenshot of a plan shows straight stacks of aligned cards.
