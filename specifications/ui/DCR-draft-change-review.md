# Draft change review

**Spec code:** `DCR`

## Intent
The surface on which an author decides what to do with the changes an agent has proposed to their draft's prompt. The changes are decided **in the document**: each one is decorated in the prose it changes, inside the document column of the draft's tab (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-ZRPT), and the author accepts or rejects each on its own. The author can also tell an agent which hunk to decide, by voice or in a comment, and the agent's tool (`../tools/DDC-decide-draft-changes-tool.md`) runs the operation of the matching chip; this review shows that decision as it shows any other. A rejected hunk stays in the document until the proposal closes, so the author can still accept it, and any hunk can be talked about in a discussion of its own. It exists because a proposed change is only reviewable in the text it belongs to — a rewrite read away from its context is read without the thing that decides whether it is right — and because a single objection should hold up one change rather than every change offered with it. Each change is a **hunk**: an anchored replacement, insertion, or deletion, carrying its own state and its own decision. A hunk's proposed text is the author's to correct before they take it, so a change that is right but for one word is not declined and asked for again. Accepting one hunk writes the prompt and records one version in the draft's own History (per `NAW-new-artifact.md` NAW-FR-36); rejecting one writes nothing. Because the work that produced a proposal outlives the glance that started it, this surface also **calls the author back to it**: a proposal recorded while they are looking at something else raises a notification that lands them on the draft with the changes in front of them. This review is also the **reference** for every other in-place review of an agent's changes: the review of a published prompt or a project document in its Editor tab (`PCR-prompt-change-review.md`) applies its presentation unchanged, so an author learns one review for drafts and for files. Out of scope: the two-column shape the review is rendered inside, the discussion beside it, and the scrolling of either, all of which are `DDS-draft-discussion.md`'s; the proposal's storage, its hunks, its anchors, and the one-undecided-proposal-per-draft rule, owned by `../core/DCP-draft-change-proposals.md`; the transaction an acceptance is performed as, owned by `../core/DHS-draft-history.md`, which this surface invokes through one operation per hunk and never coordinates itself; the draft's History rail, which is `NAW-new-artifact.md`'s; composing a proposal, which is an agent's and reaches the author through `../tools/PDC-propose-draft-changes-tool.md`; the comment a proposal and its decision each take in the conversation, which `CMT-comments.md` renders and `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` holds; and deciding what is worth interrupting for, which is `NTF-notifications.md`'s. The same surface reviews a **decomposition**: an agent proposes, one at a time, the new drafts that replace a draft that grew too big (`../tools/PND-propose-new-draft-tool.md`), and each proposal is a **new-draft card** in the discussion. The author accepts, rejects, discusses, or previews each one on its card, and only the author ends the decomposition, with **Confirm decomposition done** or by telling an agent to confirm it (`../tools/DDC-decide-draft-changes-tool.md`).

## Functional requirements
1. **DCR-FR-01** The review renders **inside the document column** of the draft's New Artifact tab. It is no modal, no scrim, and none of the window's mutually-exclusive floating overlays (per `SNV-shell-navigation.md` SNV-FR-56): opening it closes no overlay and no overlay closes it.
2. **DCR-FR-02** A draft holds at most one undecided proposal (per `../core/DCP-draft-change-proposals.md` DCP-FR-04), and the review shows that proposal. The prompt stays editable while it stands.
3. **DCR-FR-03** A proposal that arrives while its draft's tab is open enters review in place, with nothing for the author to activate. A New Artifact tab whose draft holds an undecided proposal also renders an indication in its action row, which scrolls the document to the first undecided hunk.
4. **DCR-FR-04** The proposal's `rationale` is read in the discussion, as the body of the agent's own comment (per `../tools/PDC-propose-draft-changes-tool.md` PDC-FR-12). The review states no rationale of its own.
   - *Why:* One text read in one place; a second copy beside the hunks would drift from the comment an agent later reads.
5. **DCR-FR-05** The review renders the prompt **as it stands** with each hunk decorated in place. Each hunk is placed and decorated by its **kind**, so what is drawn is what accepting does. The hunks and their resolved positions come from `"load draft change proposal hunks"`, and the surface derives no hunk and no comparison of its own.
6. **DCR-FR-06** A hunk is resolved against the prompt as it stands rather than as the agent read it, so the review always shows what accepting will do — including where the author has since edited the text around it.
7. **DCR-FR-07** Proposed text renders on `--diff-add-bg` behind a rail of `--ok`. Text a hunk removes renders on `--diff-del-bg` behind `--danger`, struck through. A replacement renders both, a deletion the removed text alone, and an insertion the proposed text alone. A hunk held for discussion, and a rejected hunk of an open proposal, render on `--warn-soft` behind `--warn`. An accepted hunk and a withdrawn hunk render no decoration.
8. **DCR-FR-08** The hunks are fetched when the tab opens on a draft that holds a proposal, and are re-read when `"draft change proposals changed"` reports one. A draft holding no proposal costs no read.
9. **DCR-FR-09** A hunk's proposed text renders as a block **between** the document's blocks, on the page the prompt is read on. It renders its Markdown the way the page renders the prompt's own Markdown, with the Editor's construct set (per `EDT-editor.md` EDT-FR-68) and no raw HTML: a heading in it reads as a heading, a list as a list, and emphasis as emphasis, so the proposed text and the text it replaces read in one typography. It never takes the typography of the block its anchor ends in. The review offers no separate rendering of it and no comparison mode.
   - *Why:* The removed text is the document's own text and renders rich where it stands. Proposed text shown as Markdown source beside it makes the author compare two notations rather than two versions of one passage.
10. **DCR-FR-10** Each hunk carries an accessible name naming its position in the proposal, its kind, and the proposing agent, and, for a rejected hunk, that it is rejected. A hunk reached by keyboard is so identified without its decoration.
11. **DCR-FR-11** A **review bar** at the foot of the document column, below the scroller, names the hunk under review and the number of hunks the proposal holds that are not withdrawn, and carries a control that moves the review to the **previous** hunk, one that moves it to the **next**, **Reject all**, and **Accept all**. The two move controls stand with the counter they move — the counter names which hunk of how many, and the controls are how the author changes it — and each names the accelerator of DCR-FR-30 that does the same thing, so the accelerator is learned from the control rather than having to be known first. They move the review by DCR-FR-30's rule, and are the **primary** way through a proposal, the two whole-proposal actions staying the convenience path of DCR-FR-CXZG: a surface offering only those two reads as a proposal to take or leave whole, which is the opposite of what deciding hunk by hunk exists for. A move rounds both ends by DCR-FR-30's rule, so neither control stops at the last hunk or the first; a control is unavailable — rather than absent — only where there is nowhere to move, which is a proposal holding the one hunk the review visits (DCR-FR-QPRV) with the review already on it. It stays in place while it is unavailable, so the bar keeps its shape and the counter beside it does not move under the author's pointer. It holds its place there while the document scrolls. The foot is where it belongs because it is a set of controls the author acts with rather than a heading the document is read under: it sits where the hand already is, and it does not stand between the formatting toolbar and the first line of the prompt.
12. **DCR-FR-12** The hunk under review carries an action chip, which covers no line. An undecided hunk's chip holds **Accept**, **Reject**, and **Discuss**. A rejected hunk's chip states `Rejected` in words and holds **Accept** and **Discuss**. The review opens on the first undecided hunk. A decision moves it to the next undecided hunk, else the first, else to none.
13. **DCR-FR-QPRV** While the proposal is open, the previous and next moves (DCR-FR-11, DCR-FR-30) visit every undecided and every rejected hunk, in proposal order. They never visit an accepted hunk.
13. **DCR-FR-13** **Accept** invokes `"accept draft change hunk"` for that hunk alone. On success the prompt holds that change, the draft's History gains one version marked **Latest accepted** (per `NAW-new-artifact.md` NAW-FR-42), and every other hunk is untouched.
14. **DCR-FR-14** **Reject** invokes `"reject draft change hunk"` for that hunk alone. The prompt does not change, the draft's History gains nothing, and every other hunk is untouched.
15. **DCR-FR-15** After a decision of this surface that **resolves** the proposal, the surface dispatches `"dispatch agent turn"` once for each distinct agent of the conversation's active set, and not at all where that set is empty (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-LCFU, CTA-FR-QUXJ). An unresolving decision dispatches nothing, and so does a decision by tool (`../tools/DDC-decide-draft-changes-tool.md` DDC-FR-KQZN).
16. **DCR-FR-16** A decision the backend refuses renders its typed error beside the hunk it names, leaves the prompt byte-for-byte what it was, and leaves every hunk state unchanged. The decision is retried without retyping anything.
17. **DCR-FR-17** A proposal closes when no hunk is undecided (per `../core/DCP-draft-change-proposals.md` DCP-FR-PWSF). A closed proposal leaves the document undecorated and the review bar gone, and its hunk discussions stay readable. What was decided is read in the conversation, where the offer and the answer stand in sequence (per `../core/DCP-draft-change-proposals.md` DCP-FR-15).
18. **DCR-FR-18** This surface mounts a subscriber to `"draft change proposals changed"` at the **shell** rather than in the tab, and raises through `NTF-notifications.md` (NTF-FR-01) when a proposal is recorded. The raise carries the level Info and the `draft/<draft-id>` address of NTF-FR-03. Nothing is raised when a hunk is decided or held.
    - *Why:* A tab-scoped listener hears nothing for a draft whose tab is closed, which is the case the author most needs telling about.
19. **DCR-FR-19** Whether the raise becomes a notification is `NTF-notifications.md`'s policy (NTF-FR-02, NTF-FR-08). Activating it opens or focuses that draft's tab and scrolls the document to the first undecided hunk.
20. **DCR-FR-20** Acceptance is **per hunk**. Accepting one hunk leaves every other hunk undecided, and the author accepts some hunks of a proposal and rejects others.
21. **DCR-FR-SRPZ** A decision by tool reaches the review through `"draft change proposals changed"`, and the review follows it as a decision from another window (DCR-FR-21). Where the decided hunk was under review, the review moves by the rule of DCR-FR-12. A proposal that the tool closes ends the review (DCR-FR-17).
21. **DCR-FR-KHEE** An acceptance by tool that lands while the draft's tab is open updates the prompt and the History rail as `NAW-new-artifact.md` NAW-FR-42 states. A hunk discussion that the tool opens shows in the discussion column as any new discussion of the draft does, and the review shows the hunk as held where it was undecided.
21. **DCR-FR-21** The surface follows `"draft change proposals changed"` while it is showing: a hunk decided from another window clears its decoration, and a proposal resolved elsewhere ends the review. Any unwritten edit to a hunk decided elsewhere is dropped with it.
22. **DCR-FR-22** The review ends with its tab and with the tab's draft: closing the tab, deleting the draft, graduating the draft, and changing the project's active worktree (per `TAB-tabs.md` TAB-FR-14) each end it. The proposal is untouched and the review resumes when the draft is opened again.
23. **DCR-FR-23** On this surface, nothing but **Accept**, **Reject**, **Accept all**, and **Reject all** decides anything. The one other decider is the tool of `../core/DCP-draft-change-proposals.md` DCP-FR-CIPW, on the author's instruction. Scrolling away from a hunk, closing the tab, and pressing Escape each decide no hunk and change no file of the draft.
24. **DCR-FR-24** While a hunk is undecided its proposed text is **editable**, on the terms `DFV-diff-viewer.md` sets for a target (DFV-FR-41) and with the Editor's Markdown editing semantics (per `EDT-editor.md` EDT-FR-17). It is edited **in place**, in the rendering DCR-FR-09 gives it, with the editing behaviour of the Editor's WYSIWYG surface, and an edit is held as the Markdown that surface writes. The prompt's own text around the hunk stays editable as it always is.
25. **DCR-FR-25** An edited hunk is held **keyed by its hunk** rather than by the surface, so it survives the tab being closed and the draft reopened. It is dropped when that hunk is decided, and when the draft is deleted, graduated, or put out of reach by a project or worktree change.
26. **DCR-FR-26** A hunk edit schedules `"edit draft change hunk"` a short rest after the last keystroke, and is brought forward when the hunk is decided and when the tab closes. **That write is not a change to the draft**: it touches no file under the draft's `files/` and leaves the draft's `updated_at` where it was.
27. **DCR-FR-27** The surface states, in words, which of the two things has happened. A hunk whose text the author has edited says so and says that the draft has not changed. It says the draft has changed only after an acceptance succeeds, and both statements are announced to assistive technology.
28. **DCR-FR-28** Every hunk, its three actions, and every control of the review bar are reachable and operable by keyboard alone. Each state a hunk carries is conveyed by more than colour.
29. **DCR-FR-29** A hunk is never replaced under an unresolved edit. A `"edit draft change hunk"` refused because the stored text has moved on (per `../core/DCP-draft-change-proposals.md` DCP-FR-27) raises an explicit conflict offering to keep what is on screen or to take what storage holds, and disables that hunk's decisions until one is chosen.
30. **DCR-FR-30** One accelerator moves the review to the previous hunk and another to the next, scrolling the **document** to it. No accelerator accepts or rejects a hunk: only the controls of DCR-FR-23 do. The move accelerators do nothing while the caret is in a text field of the discussion column.
    - *Why:* Enter and Backspace are typing keys, so an accelerator on either would decide a change while the author writes a message or a paragraph.
31. **DCR-FR-31** A hunk the backend reports as **lost** states that the text it changes is no longer in the prompt, renders **Accept** disabled with that reason, and keeps **Reject** enabled. A hunk the review **cannot draw in the document** is a different thing and is stated as one: it is still named by the review bar, still counted, and still decided, and while it is the hunk under review the bar says that it is not drawn. Its **Accept** and **Reject** both stay available, a hunk the surface cannot draw being one the backend can still decide, and rejecting being how the author clears it. A surface that counts a hunk it does not show, and answers a move to that hunk with nothing changing on screen, reports a defect of its own as though it were an ordinary state — and leaves the author unable to tell the two apart, or to tell either from a control that does not work.
    - *Why:* Rejecting is the only act left that clears such a hunk, so disabling both would leave the proposal undecidable.
32. **DCR-FR-32** Everything this surface says about a proposal it says from **one reading per draft**, taken with `"list draft change proposals"` when a surface first needs it and kept current from `"draft change proposals changed"` thereafter. A reading that failed is asked for again rather than reported as an absence.
33. **DCR-FR-33** The readings of DCR-FR-32 belong to the content root they were taken from, and are discarded when the project closes and when the active worktree changes.
34. **DCR-FR-BQNL** The document scroller carries a **gutter map** on its outer edge marking every hunk the review draws by kind, so a hunk outside the viewport stays visible as a mark.
35. **DCR-FR-HVXK** The agent's proposal comment carries a summary of the hunk count and the lines the proposal adds and removes, and an action that scrolls the document column to the first undecided hunk.
35. **DCR-FR-OYRR** An update comment's `proposal` reference (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-BSQB) carries, in place of that summary, one line naming the number of hunks the update changed, added, and withdrawn. Its action scrolls the document column to the first undecided hunk, and where no hunk is undecided it states no route.
36. **DCR-FR-MTFD** **Discuss** on a hunk, undecided or rejected, opens the composer of a **new** discussion of the draft in the discussion column (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-RHKW). Its text starts with the proposing agent's tag, and it carries that hunk's reference. Posting invokes `"open discussion (target, fragment target, body, attachments)"` with one `proposal_hunk` attachment naming the hunk.
37. **DCR-FR-JTXE** After a hunk discussion opens, the surface invokes `"hold draft change hunk"` for that hunk where it is undecided. A rejected hunk stays rejected. Opening, resolving, and reopening a hunk discussion decide no hunk.
38. **DCR-FR-VBQN** A **hunk discussion** is a discussion whose opening comment carries a `proposal_hunk` naming that hunk. Where one exists, **Discuss** shows it in the discussion column instead of opening a second, a resolved one included.
37. **DCR-FR-WRJP** An update of the open proposal redraws the review in place. A changed hunk keeps its identity and is undecided, a rejected hunk included, and an unwritten edit of it is dropped. An added hunk is drawn and counted in proposal order, and a withdrawn hunk leaves the document.
37. **DCR-FR-OZVU** A `withdrawn` hunk is hidden in review: it has no decoration, no gutter mark, and no chip, the review bar does not count it, and no move visits it. Its hunk discussions stay readable.
    - *Why:* The agent took the change back, so nothing about it is the author's to decide.
37. **DCR-FR-NYDA** The surface offers no decision and no edit on a withdrawn hunk. A `hunk_withdrawn` the backend returns for a decision made from a stale reading renders as a refused decision does (DCR-FR-16).
37. **DCR-FR-QXFU** An update that leaves the proposal open raises as a recording does (DCR-FR-18, DCR-FR-19), with the same key, so it replaces any earlier raise of that proposal and lands on the first undecided hunk. An update that closes the proposal raises nothing. The subscriber reads the event's `cause` to tell an update from a decision or a hold.
38. **DCR-FR-CXZG** **Accept all** invokes `"accept draft change hunk"` once for each undecided hunk, in order, and stops at the first refusal, leaving the hunks after it undecided. **Reject all** invokes `"decline draft change proposal"` once.
    - *Why:* Each acceptance is its own transaction, so a partial run must leave the author looking at exactly what did and did not land.
39. **DCR-FR-KDSV** A proposal the backend reports as **legacy** renders as one hunk covering the whole prompt and accepts no edit to that hunk. It is accepted and rejected on the ordinary terms.
40. **DCR-FR-LGHZ** A hunk's `before` and `lead` are Markdown source. The surface finds a hunk in the document by the text that this Markdown renders to with the Editor's own parser. A hunk that quotes a link, an escape, an entity, an autolink, a rule, a table, a task item, a hard break, or an image is drawn like any other hunk.
    - *Why:* The document holds rendered text only, so a search with the Markdown source fails for each construct that renders differently.
41. **DCR-FR-TSNW** An insertion is drawn at the end of its `lead`, and only that end must match the document. When the full `lead` is not found, the surface removes words from its start, one at a time, until the remainder is found. A remainder keeps at least three words. The occurrence nearest the hint wins.
    - *Why:* The backend cuts a `lead` at a fixed length, so its start can stop inside a link or a code span.
42. **DCR-FR-GNRL** The review surface of this spec is also the review of a published prompt or a project document in its Editor tab, on the terms of `PCR-prompt-change-review.md` PCR-FR-QHZN. There it renders in the Editor tab's document column, with the operations and the event of `../core/PCP-prompt-change-proposals.md` in place of the draft's.

43. **DCR-FR-RDLK** A comment that carries a `new_draft_proposal` attachment (per `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md` CMS-FR-YKGI) renders a **new-draft card** in the discussion stream, below the comment's body. The card reads its proposal from one reading per draft, taken with `"list new draft proposals"` and kept current from `"new draft proposals changed"`.
44. **DCR-FR-QWXP** The card's header names `Part N of M`, the proposed name, and the state in words: `Waiting for you`, `Accepted`, `Rejected`, or `Withdrawn`. An accepted card also names the draft it created. Each state has its own marker as well as its own colour.
45. **DCR-FR-MGMZ** The card of a pending proposal holds **Accept**, **Reject**, **Discuss**, **Preview**, and an optional one-line feedback field. Where several comments name the same proposal, the newest one carries these controls, and each earlier one renders a one-line reference that names the update it made.
46. **DCR-FR-UTKG** **Accept** invokes `"accept new draft proposal"` with the feedback where the author typed some. On success the card reads `Accepted` and names the created draft. The original draft does not change.
47. **DCR-FR-FMRQ** **Reject** invokes `"reject new draft proposal"` with the feedback where the author typed some. On success the card reads `Rejected`, and no draft is created.
48. **DCR-FR-NMJH** After a decision of this surface that carries feedback, the surface dispatches `"dispatch agent turn"` once for each distinct agent of the conversation's active set, with the decision comment as the trigger (per `CTA-comment-agent-turns.md` CTA-FR-LCFU). A decision without feedback dispatches nothing.
49. **DCR-FR-KNDA** While no proposal of the set is pending, the decomposition is not confirmed, and no turn of the conversation runs, a **What next?** row stands below the newest decided new-draft card of the conversation. It holds **Ask for next draft**, and **Confirm decomposition done** where at least one proposal of the set is accepted.
50. **DCR-FR-UVMY** **Ask for next draft** dispatches `"dispatch agent turn"` once for each distinct agent of the conversation's active set, with the newest decision comment as the trigger, or to the agent of the newest decided proposal where that set is empty. It records nothing and decides nothing.
51. **DCR-FR-BELO** **Confirm decomposition done** invokes `"confirm draft decomposition"` and asks for no confirmation of its own. On success the What next? row gives way to the statement `Decomposition confirmed`, which names the accepted drafts. It dispatches no agent turn.
52. **DCR-FR-WRQT** Where no proposal of the set is accepted, **Confirm decomposition done** is not offered anywhere, and the original draft stays as it is. No surface but the What next? row offers it.
53. **DCR-FR-AMLR** **Discuss** opens the composer of a new discussion of the original draft in the discussion column, its text starting with the proposing agent's tag. Posting invokes `"open discussion (target, fragment target, body, attachments)"` with one `new_draft_subject` attachment naming the proposal (CMS-FR-KAXQ). Where such a discussion exists, Discuss shows it instead.
54. **DCR-FR-HWLA** **Preview** opens a **draft preview tab** (per `TAB-tabs.md` TAB-FR-CIGU) that shows the proposed body from `"load new draft proposal body"`, rendered read-only as a New Artifact tab renders its prompt. Its label is the proposed name. It creates no draft and no file.
55. **DCR-FR-RJZP** The draft preview tab re-reads the body when `"new draft proposals changed"` reports an update of its proposal, and closes when its proposal is decided or withdrawn.
56. **DCR-FR-TOOT** A decision or a confirmation the backend refuses renders its typed error on the card or the row, changes no state, and keeps the typed feedback, so the author retries without retyping anything.
57. **DCR-FR-FKNQ** Every control of the card and of the What next? row is reachable and operable by keyboard alone. The card's accessible name reads `Proposed draft, part N of M, <name>, <state>`.
58. **DCR-FR-QQLE** The shell subscriber of DCR-FR-18 also follows `"new draft proposals changed"` and raises through `NTF-notifications.md` (NTF-FR-01) at level Info, with the `draft/<draft-id>` address, when a proposal is recorded or updated and stays pending. Activating the raise opens that draft's tab and shows the card.
59. **DCR-FR-THXH** A decision by tool (`../tools/DDC-decide-draft-changes-tool.md`) reaches the card through `"new draft proposals changed"`, and the card follows it as a decision from another window. It dispatches no turn.
60. **DCR-FR-PBDD** While the original draft is archived, graduated, held by a graduation run, or a GitHub-shadow draft, the card's **Accept** and **Reject** and the row's **Confirm decomposition done** are disabled, each with the reason stated beside it.
61. **DCR-FR-LFDJ** The new-draft card is drawn as the question card of the discussion stream is drawn: the same frame, type chip, author line, and time. Its type chip reads `NEW`.
62. **DCR-FR-KYJS** A confirmation by tool reaches the discussion through `"new draft proposals changed"` with cause `confirmed` (DCP-FR-ZDSP). The What next? row then gives way to the statement `Decomposition confirmed`, which names the accepted drafts, as on the row's own confirmation (DCR-FR-BELO). A decision by tool on a new-draft proposal changes its card as the card's own decision does.

## User stories
- As an author whose draft grew too big, I want the agent to propose the new drafts one at a time, so that I decide each one with the earlier ones in mind.
- As an author, I want to read a proposed draft as a prompt before I accept it.
- As an author, I want to be the one who says the decomposition is done.
- As an author, I want to read a proposed change in the passage it changes, so that accepting is a judgement rather than a hope.
- As an author, I want to accept the changes I agree with and keep discussing the rest, so that one objection does not block the others.
- As an author, I want to talk about one change in a discussion of its own, so that the talk about it does not bury the rest of the conversation.
- As an author who rejected a change too fast, I want to accept it while the proposal is still open.
- As an author looking at a change that is right but for one word, I want to fix that word and accept it, so that a good rewrite is not thrown away over a detail.
- As an author, I want to be sure my draft has not changed until I accept, and to be told plainly which of the two has happened.
- As an author working in another tab, I want to be told when an agent has something for me to look at.

## Wireframes

Three hunks in the prose, the second under review:

```
│  ## Ontology                                                        ▍│
│  This prompt is structured around the protocol ontology first…      ▍│
│                                                                      │
│  ┃−  A user is a person who uses the IDE.            (struck through)▍│
│    ┌─[ 2/3   [ Accept ]  [ Reject ]   Discuss → ]──────┐             │
│  ┃+  A USER is a person who uses the IDE and the       │  (bold)    ▍│
│  ┃   remote client. A user may own projects and drafts.│             │
│    └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘             ▍│
│                                                                      │
│  ┃+  CAPABILITY                                         (heading)   ▍│  ← hunk 3, pending
```

- Layout notes: the review bar sits above the page and holds its place while the document scrolls, so the change counter and the two whole-proposal actions are reachable without scrolling to either end. The action chip is placed over padding the hunk reserves for it — above an insertion, below a deletion, and at the foot of the insertion of a replace pair — so it never covers a line of the document. A replace renders as its deletion immediately above its insertion, sharing one chip and one decision. The gutter map rides the outer edge of the scroller; a mark for a hunk far from the viewport is drawn back, so distance reads without a second colour. A hunk that is lost renders its statement in place of its chip's **Accept**, so the reason stands where the action would have been. A rejected hunk keeps its decoration in the held tone until the proposal closes, and its chip holds **Accept** and **Discuss** beside the word `Rejected`.

- Layout notes: the card stands in the stream below its comment's body and takes the stream's measure. The feedback field and the actions stand on the card of a pending proposal alone. The What next? row is no card: it is a quiet row below the newest decided card.

## UI contract boundary
- **Owned by the UI**: the hunk decorations and the treatment of each kind and state; the review bar, the change counter, and the two whole-proposal actions; the hunk under review, the accelerators that move it, and the guard that keeps them out of the discussion column's text fields; the action chip, its placement, and the space a hunk reserves for it; the gutter map; the editing of a hunk's proposed text, the buffer that holds it between openings, and the schedule on which it is written; the statement a lost or a legacy hunk carries; the proposal summary rendered on the agent's comment; the new-draft card, its feedback field, the What next? row, and the rendering of the draft preview tab; the hunk discussion Discuss opens and the reveal of one that exists; the inline rendering of every refusal below; the one reading per draft; the summary rendered on an update comment; and the raise made when a proposal is recorded or updated, together with the key and the address it carries.
- **Delegated to backend (abstract)**:
  - `"open discussion (target, fragment target, body, attachments)"` — the hunk discussion, owned by `../core/CMS-comments-storage.md`; it carries one `proposal_hunk` attachment (CMS-FR-TQVB).
  - `"list draft change proposals"`, `"load draft change proposal hunks"`, `"accept draft change hunk"`, `"reject draft change hunk"`, `"hold draft change hunk"`, `"edit draft change hunk"`, and `"decline draft change proposal"` — owned by `../core/DCP-draft-change-proposals.md`.
  - `"load draft file contents"` — the prompt the hunks are resolved against. Owned by `../core/DRS-draft-storage.md` (DRS-FR-12).
  - `"dispatch agent turn"` — owned by `../core/AGC-agent-conversations.md`; invoked after the decision that resolves the proposal (DCR-FR-15), after a new-draft decision that carries feedback (DCR-FR-NMJH), and by **Ask for next draft** (DCR-FR-UVMY).
  - `"list new draft proposals"`, `"load new draft proposal body"`, `"accept new draft proposal"`, `"reject new draft proposal"`, and `"confirm draft decomposition"` — owned by `../core/DCP-draft-change-proposals.md`. The event `"new draft proposals changed"` (DCP-FR-ZDSP) keeps the cards, the What next? row, and the draft preview tab current.
  - The new-draft discussion is opened through `"open discussion (target, fragment target, body, attachments)"` with one `new_draft_subject` attachment (CMS-FR-KAXQ).
  - The events `"draft change proposals changed"` (per `../core/DCP-draft-change-proposals.md` DCP-FR-16) and `"drafts changed"` (per `../core/DRS-draft-storage.md` DRS-FR-22) keep the review and the tab's indication current.
  - The raise is `NTF-notifications.md`'s facility (NTF-FR-01) and introduces no operation here.

## Non-functional requirements
- The review stays responsive on a prompt of several thousand lines and a proposal of up to 50 hunks.
- Resolving the hunks against the prompt is settled rather than run per keystroke, and moves neither the caret nor the scroll position.
- Change marking is conveyed by more than colour, so a proposal is reviewable without colour discrimination.
- Nothing in the review scrolls horizontally, and it stays usable at the minimum window size the shell supports (≥1280×800).
- Editing a hunk costs one call per settled burst of typing and never a draft write.
- A raise costs the shell one subscription and nothing per draft.
- Nothing here reaches the network beyond what the decision's own comment resolves.
