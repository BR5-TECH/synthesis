# Draft information

**Spec code:** `DFI`

## Intent
The surface that answers "what did this draft cost?". Opened from the Drafts panel's **Information** entry, it is a modal overlay showing one draft's **captured lifetime totals** and its **GitHub publication record**: the foreground time spent editing it, the AI interactions it took, the versions it settled, the proposals it decided, the tokens its conversations reported, and the time and reported tokens the agents spent on it across conversational refinement and every graduation run it was put through. Beside those totals it states where the draft has been published: the current GitHub issue and, beneath it, every earlier one. It exists because a draft is long-lived, several are in flight at once, and a graduation run is an hour of work inside a container nobody sees into — so the effort a piece of working material took is otherwise unknowable the moment the session ends. The modal is a **reading surface and nothing else**: it decides nothing, changes nothing, and starts nothing, and dismissing it leaves the draft, its conversations, its graduation runs, and its history exactly as they were. It shows aggregates alone and never a per-run breakdown, because the question it answers is about the draft rather than about any one run. The publication record is the one exception to that shape, being a list of facts rather than a total, and it is as read-only as everything else here. A total the application did not capture is stated as **unavailable**, and one whose captured record is damaged or partial is stated as **incomplete**; neither is ever rendered as a zero, because a zero is a claim and these are not. Beside the modal this spec owns the small observer that makes the editing time real: it watches the application's focus, which surfaces of this draft are visible, and whether the author is still interacting, and it reports settled foreground intervals to the backend. Out of scope: storing, folding, or deduplicating anything, all of which are `../core/DSS-draft-statistics-storage.md`'s; the context menu the entry sits in, which is `DRP-drafts-panel.md`'s (DRP-FR-10); the graduation runs themselves and every decision taken on one, which are `GRU-graduation-runs.md`'s and `GRU-graduation-runs.md`'s; the run-by-run account of a graduation, which is `GRU-graduation-runs.md`'s and is not repeated here; and editing the draft, holding its conversation, or deciding a proposal, none of which this surface offers a control for.

## User stories
- As an author deciding whether to keep refining a draft or graduate it, I want to see what it has already cost in time and agent work, so the decision rests on something.
- As an author who graduated a draft last month, I want its account still readable, so I can compare what a specification took against what the next one is taking.
- As an author whose project has only just started recording, I want to be told a figure is not captured rather than shown a zero, so I do not read an absence as a fact.

- Layout notes: the modal is a centred modal overlay using the visual primitives, spacing, and dismissal behaviour of the Project context menu (per `LCM-library-context-menu.md` LCM-FR-07), exactly as the Drafts panel's own confirmations do, and it is mutually exclusive with the other overlays of the main window on the terms of `NFI-new-file.md` NFI-FR-01. Its title is the draft's current name and its only chrome control is the dismissing `✕`; there is no confirming action, this surface having nothing to confirm. The body is one scrollable column of labelled rows in four groups in this fixed order — the five counters with the conversation token pair, then **Agent time**, then **Agent-reported tokens**, then **Published to GitHub** — each group carrying its heading and each bucket group listing **Refinement**, **Authoring**, **Validation / hand-off / publication**, **Implementation**, **Reviews**, **Reconciliation**, and **Total** in exactly that order. A value that was not captured renders as a stated phrase in place of a number rather than as a dash, an empty cell, or a zero; an incomplete value renders the number it has beside a marker whose hover and accessible name say the figure is partial. The footer states the date the draft's captured record begins on, and states instead that nothing has been captured where the draft has no boundary. **Published to GitHub** renders only where the draft holds at least one publication record: the current record under its own subheading and the earlier ones newest first under theirs, each one line of repository and issue number, one line of publication instant, and one line of marker. Nothing in the body is a control and no route leaves this modal, save the issue link of DFI-FR-WQLE, which leaves the application altogether.

## UI contract boundary
- **Owned by the UI**: the modal's rendering as a centred overlay, its title, its dismissal, and its mutual exclusion with the other overlays; the three groups, their order, the fixed bucket order within the two bucket groups, and the row labels; the formatting of a duration, of a count, and of a token figure; the rendering of the unavailable and the incomplete states and the accessible names that carry them; the footer sentence and the formatting of the boundary date; the decision to re-read on `"draft statistics changed"`; the observer of DFI-FR-XKRM — which surfaces count as related, when the application is considered focused, what counts as an interaction, the five-minute inactivity threshold, and when an interval is settled and reported; and the fact that this surface renders no per-run breakdown and offers no control that changes anything.
- **Delegated to backend (abstract)**:
  - `"read draft statistics"` — returns the draft's captured lifetime aggregate totals, each carrying its own availability. Owned by `../core/DSS-draft-statistics-storage.md` (DSS-FR-YOVS). It is the modal's one read, it creates no telemetry activation boundary (DSS-FR-JRSY), and it is invoked when the modal opens and again on each `"draft statistics changed"` for that draft.
  - `"record draft editing interval"` — records one settled foreground editing interval for a draft. Owned by `../core/DSS-draft-statistics-storage.md` (DSS-FR-RIDW). It is invoked by the observer of DFI-FR-XKRM rather than by the modal, and a modal that is never opened changes nothing about it.
  - The event `"draft statistics changed"` (`../core/DSS-draft-statistics-storage.md` DSS-FR-TWNA) is what keeps an open modal current without polling.
  - `"get draft publication (draft id)"` — the draft's current publication, its whole history newest-first, and its standing attempt. Owned by `../core/GHP-github-publication.md` (GHP-FR-CWTG). It is invoked when the modal opens and again on each `"draft publication changed"` for that draft, and it writes nothing.
  - `"open publication issue (draft id, url)"` — opens one recorded issue URL in the system's external browser. Owned by `../core/GHP-github-publication.md` (GHP-FR-MJTB). It is the modal's one control beyond dismissal.
  - This surface invokes no draft, conversation, proposal, history, graduation, or publishing operation of any kind: it reads publication and never starts, retries, recovers, or abandons one.

## Functional requirements
1. **DFI-FR-ZGBU** Information is a **centred modal overlay** for one draft, opened by the Drafts panel's **Information** entry (per `DRP-drafts-panel.md` DRP-FR-10) and by no other route. It reads `"read draft statistics"` for that draft when it opens, renders what that call returns, and re-reads and redraws on each `"draft statistics changed"` naming that draft, so a modal left open while an agent works follows the totals rather than showing what was true when it opened.
2. **DFI-FR-PWNC** The modal opens for **every** draft, whatever its state: active, archived, `graduated`, held by a non-terminal graduation run, or reported inconsistent (per `../core/DRS-draft-storage.md` DRS-FR-15). None of those disables it, because reading what a draft cost changes nothing about the draft and is exactly the thing an author wants for one they can no longer edit.
3. **DFI-FR-LTJA** Beyond the publication group of DFI-FR-BZQN the modal shows **captured lifetime aggregate totals alone**. There is no per-run row, no per-run group, no per-conversation row, no timeline, and no event list, and no control anywhere in it names, opens, or routes to a run, a conversation, a proposal, or a history entry. Its only interactive elements are the dismissing `✕`, together with the Escape and backdrop dismissal the other overlays use, and the issue links of DFI-FR-WQLE.
4. **DFI-FR-RQVE** The body renders exactly these statistics, in this order: **Time spent editing**, **AI interactions**, **Draft edits**, **Accepted proposals**, **Rejected proposals**, **Conversation tokens** as a separate input total and output total, **Agent time**, and **Agent-reported tokens**, with the publication group of DFI-FR-BZQN last. Each is rendered from the corresponding field of the one read, and none is calculated, summed, or adjusted in the frontend.
5. **DFI-FR-MODK** **Agent time** renders six buckets and a total, in the fixed order **Refinement**, **Authoring**, **Validation / hand-off / publication**, **Implementation**, **Reviews**, **Reconciliation**, **Total**. **Agent-reported tokens** renders the same six buckets in the same order, each as a separate input and output figure, and a total of the six whose two directions carry their own availability independently. The order is fixed in the surface rather than derived from the payload, so two drafts read the same way, and **Refinement** is always first.
6. **DFI-FR-HSYB** A statistic the backend reports **unavailable** renders as a stated phrase saying it was not captured, in place of the number, and never as `0`, `—`, a blank, or an omitted row: the row is present so the author reads that the figure is missing rather than that the work never happened. A statistic reported **incomplete** renders the value it carries together with a marker whose tooltip and accessible name state that the captured record for it is partial. A **total** whose parts are unavailable or incomplete carries the incomplete marker on the same terms, and renders the partial sum the read returned rather than a figure the surface completes for itself: the modal adds nothing, treats no unavailable bucket as a zero, and reads the total's availability from the payload (per `../core/DSS-draft-statistics-storage.md` DSS-FR-IYRG, DSS-FR-CMTP). A total whose every part is unavailable renders as not captured like any other unavailable figure.
7. **DFI-FR-CVAX** The modal stays fully usable when any statistic is unavailable or incomplete, and when every one of them is: it renders, scrolls, and dismisses normally, states each missing figure, and shows no error state in place of the body. A failed read is a different thing and is stated as one, with a retry, leaving the previous figures rather than replacing them with zeros.
8. **DFI-FR-NTPG** The footer states the instant the draft's captured record begins at, from the `boundary_at` the read returns, and states instead that nothing has been captured for this draft where that field is null. Opening the modal creates no boundary and records no event of any kind (per `../core/DSS-draft-statistics-storage.md` DSS-FR-VCTQ), so a draft whose Information is opened before anything was captured still has none afterwards and still reads every statistic as unavailable.
9. **DFI-FR-EBIL** Dismissing the modal — by `✕`, by Escape, or by the backdrop — closes it and changes nothing anywhere: no draft record, prompt, status, or folder; no conversation, thread, or comment; no proposal and no decision; no graduation run or queue position; no publication record or attempt; and no history entry. Nothing about the modal is persisted, so reopening it reads the totals again rather than restoring a view.
10. **DFI-FR-XKRM** The **editing-activity observer** is what produces the time this modal shows, and it runs whether or not the modal is open. It holds at most one open interval per draft. It **opens** an interval for a draft when all three of these hold: the application has focus; a **related surface** of that draft is visible — its New Artifact tab (per `NAW-new-artifact.md`), or a conversation linked to it that is shown in any surface (per `CVP-conversation-presentation.md`); and the author has interacted with the application. Agent processing counts inside an open interval, an agent composing an answer being work on that draft. It **settles** the interval and reports it through `"record draft editing interval"` on any of three conditions: the application loses focus, no related surface is visible any longer, or **five minutes pass with no author interaction with the application**. The interval **stays open through the whole of that idle period and ends at the five-minute mark**, not at the last interaction before it: an author reading what is on screen without touching anything is working, so the threshold is what decides they have stopped rather than the last keystroke, and the time up to the threshold is counted while the time after it is not. It **reopens** a new interval when the author interacts again while a related surface is visible and the application has focus, so a return after an hour away opens a second interval rather than extending the settled one; an interaction that arrives before the threshold is reached settles nothing and simply restarts the five minutes with the interval still open. A conversation counts only while the application has focus.
11. **DFI-FR-UKFR** The observer reports the **whole of each settled interval** and reconstructs nothing. It knows nothing of a draft's telemetry activation boundary and applies none: an interval that opened before that boundary and settled after it is reported whole and contributes its post-boundary part alone, the clip being the backend fold's (per `../core/DSS-draft-statistics-storage.md` DSS-FR-KYTB). It reports settled intervals alone and never derives an interval from the number of times a tab or this modal was opened, from a surface's mount, or from the age of a draft. An interval it never settled — the application closing mid-interval among them — is reported not at all rather than guessed at, and the statistic it would have contributed to is smaller by exactly that interval rather than wrong about it.
12. **DFI-FR-YAOM** A conversation counts as related for a draft when its origin is `draft_comment` or `draft_discussion` and carries that draft's id (per `../core/AGC-agent-conversations.md` AGC-FR-05). An artifact conversation, a note discussion, and a discussion about a specification a graduation published are not related, and a conversation shown in its owner's surface or in a conversation tab neither opens a second interval nor multiplies one: a surface is where a conversation is being read rather than how many conversations there are (per `CVP-conversation-presentation.md`).
13. **DFI-FR-JDWS** Two related surfaces visible at once — the draft's tab beside its discussion column — hold **one** interval rather than two, so time is counted once however many of a draft's surfaces the author has in front of them. Related surfaces of **two different drafts** visible at once hold one interval each, one per draft, because the author is at both.
14. **DFI-FR-GQTZ** The modal renders no draft content of any kind: no prompt text, no comment, no proposal, no rationale, no agent output, and no run log. It renders instants, counts, durations, token figures, publication records, and the draft's name, and nothing else.
15. **DFI-FR-BZQN** The **Published to GitHub** group renders from `"get draft publication (draft id)"`, read when the modal opens and re-read on each `"draft publication changed"` naming this draft. It renders the **current** record first under its own subheading, then every earlier record **newest first**, each carrying the repository, the issue number, the issue URL, the publication instant, and the marker. A record that holds a publication choice also states whether the issue was published as a root issue or as a sub-issue of a parent issue number, and the Type and the milestone where the record holds them.
16. **DFI-FR-TRXG** The group is **absent** where the draft holds no publication record. An absent group is not an unavailable statistic and carries none of the wording of DFI-FR-HSYB, because a draft nobody published is not a draft whose publication was uncaptured.
17. **DFI-FR-WQLE** Each record's issue URL is activatable and invokes `"open publication issue (draft id, url)"`, which opens the issue in the system's external browser. It opens no in-application page, tab, or overlay, and it leaves the modal open on the draft it was reading.
18. **DFI-FR-KAVX** The publication group is **read-only**. The modal offers no control to publish, retry, recover, abandon, edit, reorder, or remove a record, and a standing publication attempt is not acted on here — it is rendered in the draft's own tab instead (per `NAW-new-artifact.md` NAW-FR-LQAF).

## Non-functional requirements
- The modal costs one backend read on open and one per change event, and never a read per run, per conversation, or per event, so a draft with a long history opens as fast as a new one.
- The observer costs the shell's existing focus, visibility, and interaction signals and adds no timer beyond the single inactivity timer per open interval, so a session with no draft surface visible does no work at all.
- The observer's report is fire-and-forget: a failed or slow `"record draft editing interval"` never delays the author, blocks a surface, or produces a visible error, on the terms `../core/DSS-draft-statistics-storage.md` DSS-FR-TUMX sets for every recording path.
- Every figure carries an accessible name that includes its label and its state, so a screen reader reads "Reconciliation, not captured" rather than an unlabelled blank.
- The modal is keyboard-operable: it takes focus when it opens, traps focus while it is open, returns focus to the row that opened it when it closes, and dismisses on Escape.
