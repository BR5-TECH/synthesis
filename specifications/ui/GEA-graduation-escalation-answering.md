# Graduation escalation answering

**Spec code:** `GEA`

## Intent
Where the author answers work that has stopped to ask them something. A graduation run rests in `awaiting_author`, a merge run included, and a work stream update rests `escalated`, each carrying a reason and an ordered set of questions; this is the surface that renders them and takes the answers back. It is answered one question at a time with pagination in both directions, because a set of questions presented at once is a form rather than a conversation, and because an author who has answered six of seven should be able to see which one is still open. What the author has entered and not yet sent is held against the run's own questions, so paging, a re-read of the run, a switch to another run and back, and a refusal of the whole set all leave the work where it was. Out of scope: what raised the escalation and what the loop does with the answers, which are `../core/GRD-graduation.md`'s, `../ai/GRL-graduation-loop.md`'s and `../core/GRB-graduation-rebase.md`'s; the rail and the run region a run's dialog is rendered inside, which are `GRU-graduation-runs.md`'s; the window an update's is rendered inside, which is `WSS-work-stream-selector.md`'s; and any judgement of the answers, since the set is carried back whole and unread.

## UI contract boundary
- **Owned by the UI**: the rendering of the reason and of every recorded question in its recorded order; the affected paths and the boundary a semantic-rebase escalation of an update names, and the affected paths and the publication choice a merge run's escalation names; each question's proposed responses and free-text field as rows of one answer group; what counts as answered; the pagination and what it moves; the enablement of the send control and the reason it states while unavailable; the in-panel dialog's placement, measure, and treatment; and the unsent answer draft — what it is keyed by, what it holds, what it survives, and when it is dropped.
- **Delegated to backend (abstract)**: `answer_graduation_escalation` — owned by `../core/GRD-graduation.md` (GXD-FR-BJYT) — and `"answer work stream update escalation (id, answers)"` — owned by `../core/WKS-work-streams.md` (WKS-FR-CBXW). Exactly one of them is invoked, once, with the whole ordered set. A run's escalation, a merge run's included, goes through the first; an update's goes through the second. This surface introduces no operation of its own, and reads what it renders from the operations `GRU-graduation-runs.md` and `WSS-work-stream-selector.md` already invoke.

## Functional requirements
1. **GEA-FR-SXXB** A run in `awaiting_author` renders the escalation's `reason` and every question it recorded, exactly as they were recorded and in the order they were recorded (per `../tools/ESU-escalate-to-user-tool.md` ESU-FR-12), whichever route raised it.
2. **GEA-FR-ZRGP** An escalation a **semantic-rebase turn** raised additionally names the **affected paths** its record carries, as project-relative paths rather than as a count, together with the publication boundary the reconciliation stands at. A run's escalation reads both from the run (per `../core/GRD-graduation.md` GXD-FR-XPUR); an update's reads them from its update record (per `../core/WKS-work-streams.md` WKS-FR-ZKUP).
    - *Why:* A decision between two versions of a requirement is a decision about named files, and an author cannot take it without knowing which.
3. **GEA-FR-UIEA** Those paths are read text on the terms `GRU-graduation-runs.md` sets for every path that surface renders, and they open nothing.
4. **GEA-FR-DNBI** Each question carries its own proposed responses — up to three, each rendered as its `summary` for a short label with its `description` as supporting text — and its own free-text field.
5. **GEA-FR-ZSDY** The proposed responses and the free-text field are rows of **one** answer group rather than a list with a field beside it, the free-text row reading as the author's own words and carrying the field inside it.
6. **GEA-FR-ZGHY** A question recorded with no proposed responses has that row and no other, so it renders the field alone with nothing to choose between.
7. **GEA-FR-BHKU** A proposed response's `description` is display-only and is submitted nowhere.
8. **GEA-FR-VWQH** Submitting invokes `"answer graduation escalation"` once with the whole ordered set, each entry naming the question's recorded position and its effective `{ answer, summary }` value, so the loop matches every answer to the question it was given for without reading a word of either.
9. **GEA-FR-HAKV** The escalation is answered one question at a time, with pagination in both directions, the author moving forward and backward through every recorded question.
10. **GEA-FR-YFMY** An answer entered on one question — a selected response, typed free text, or both — is still there when the author comes back to it, having been held in the unsent answer draft rather than sent anywhere.
11. **GEA-FR-YMLV** The area says which question of how many is showing and which of the set are answered, so the author knows what is left without paging through to find out.
12. **GEA-FR-RCUW** Moving between questions invokes nothing, and a question is never skipped, reordered, or hidden because another is unanswered.
13. **GEA-FR-TLRF** Moving takes the keyboard with it: focus lands in the question that arrived rather than staying on the control that moved it, because Previous and Next disable themselves at the two ends of the set and an author who paged to the first or last question would otherwise be left with focus on a control that had just gone.
14. **GEA-FR-HTBP** A set the author has not paged shows its **first** question.
15. **GEA-FR-NADG** A set that replaces another does the same: a run may rest on more than one escalation over its life (per `../core/GXD-graduation-execution.md` GXD-FR-HGSU, GXD-FR-XPUR), and a page belongs to the set it was turned in rather than to the surface, so a second escalation never opens on a position the author reached in the first.
16. **GEA-FR-UWLK** **What is selected is the answer.** The proposed responses and the free-text field are rows of one group and one selection stands across them, so which row the author means is recorded rather than inferred from which field holds a value.
17. **GEA-FR-UEFS** Typing in the free-text field selects its row, and selecting a proposed response leaves that row for it.
18. **GEA-FR-GCTA** Neither act clears the other's content, so an author who typed, changed their mind, and selected a response finds their words still there to change back to, and one who selected a response and then typed finds it still chosen.
19. **GEA-FR-YLWC** A question is **answered** when a proposed response is selected, or when the free-text row is selected and holds at least one non-whitespace character.
20. **GEA-FR-RVWL** The free-text row selected and empty answers nothing, and typing only spaces answers nothing.
21. **GEA-FR-TSQO** A selected response submits `{ answer: the response's answer, summary: the response's summary }` and the free-text row submits `{ answer: the text, summary: null }`, the row that is not selected submitting nothing at all.
22. **GEA-FR-NXFQ** Free text is never held to a proposed response's summary format, its summary being null by construction.
23. **GEA-FR-LRCD** A question recorded with no proposed responses has one row and no other, so its text answers it whether or not a choice was ever pressed — there is nothing else it could have meant.
24. **GEA-FR-FFKD** **Send answers** is enabled only once every recorded question is answered, and it invokes `"answer graduation escalation"` once with the whole ordered set.
25. **GEA-FR-FZMW** The surface states how many questions still need an answer while any do, so a disabled control is never a mystery.
26. **GEA-FR-VIPR** Until the backend has accepted the set and published the updated run state, the run is still rendered as waiting on the author: no part of this surface shows it as resumed, running, or answered on the strength of the call having been made.
27. **GEA-FR-TEMG** A refusal renders inline against the run (per `GRU-graduation-runs.md` GRU-FR-XQVG) with every entered answer intact and every question still reachable, so the author corrects what was refused rather than typing the set again.
28. **GEA-FR-RZQX** A model's proposed answer is **clarification metadata and never an answer**: this surface preselects no response from one, prefills no free-text field from one, and treats a `Medium`, `Low`, or `Not confident` proposal as answering nothing.
29. **GEA-FR-QKDA** The author's effective answer is theirs to give and to change on every question.
30. **GEA-FR-NPUV** This surface renders no confidence indicator, that being a value a later UI contract may require and this one does not.
31. **GEA-FR-SKWP** It renders the internal field names of a proposed response nowhere — a response reads as its summary and its description and as nothing else.
32. **GEA-FR-BVMQ** A **run's** escalation, a merge run's included, is rendered inside the run region as an in-panel dialog belonging to the affected run: it is no floating overlay of the window and no application modal (per `SNV-shell-navigation.md` SNV-FR-56), it takes no focus trap, and the rest of the queue and the rest of the window stay operable while it stands.
33. **GEA-FR-GWTH** Its question area carries a background of its own, distinct from the panel's and white in neither theme, and every text in it keeps its contrast against the application theme in each of its three settings (per `GLS-global-settings.md` GLS-FR-04).
34. **GEA-FR-JEOX** It is the reading matter of the region rather than more of the run's own report: every text in it is set at least one step above the chrome's caption size.
35. **GEA-FR-DPTT** The area is held to a **measure** and centred in the region rather than filled to the region's width — a question set across a window's width is a line the eye loses its place in, and a choice list stranded beside an arm's length of nothing — and the measure is expressed as a multiple of the UI role's own size so it moves with the reader's chosen font (per `OVW-overview.md` OVW-FR-13).
36. **GEA-FR-IHIZ** What the author has entered and not yet sent is an **unsent answer draft** held by this surface, and nothing short of sending it replaces it.
37. **GEA-FR-XTUE** The draft is keyed by the run's id together with each question's recorded position — never by the question's text and never by the index of the page showing.
38. **GEA-FR-LWQD** This surface serves a **work stream update's** escalation on the same terms as a run's: it renders the recorded reason and question set from the update record, and sends the whole ordered set through the operation that owns that record. An update's unsent answer draft is keyed by the **stream's** id together with each question's recorded position, as GEA-FR-XTUE keys a run's.
40. **GEA-FR-BZQN** An update's escalation is rendered inside the update resolution window (per `WSS-work-stream-selector.md` WSS-FR-ZMPC) rather than in the run region, belonging to a stream and to no run (per `../core/GRB-graduation-rebase.md` GRB-FR-KZUN). A merge run's escalation is never rendered there.
41. **GEA-FR-FJPG** It holds **every part** of what was entered — which row of the answer group is selected, the proposed response last chosen, and the free text — so a re-render restores exactly what the author left rather than the effective answer computed from it.
42. **GEA-FR-YWZC** A row selected with nothing written in it is a part like any other and is held as one, so the choice the author pressed stays pressed.
43. **GEA-FR-FHPX** Nothing renders a response that was left for the free-text row while that row holds the selection: reselecting it is the author's own act, and the draft keeps it so that a re-render invents no value and a re-read that still offers it changes nothing.
44. **GEA-FR-QULY** The draft survives paging between questions, a re-list on `"graduation queue changed"`, a re-read on `"graduation run changed"`, the author selecting another run in the rail and coming back, a failed read that leaves the region empty (per `RUN-runs.md` RUN-FR-16), and a backend refusal of the whole set.
45. **GEA-FR-VBUG** A run re-read while an escalation stands is **reconciled rather than replaced**: the reason, the questions, and their proposed responses are redrawn from what came back, every draft entry whose position the re-read run still records is kept against that position, and a draft entry for a position the run no longer records is dropped with it.
46. **GEA-FR-KTME** The draft is discarded when the escalation it belongs to is answered and the backend has accepted the set, and when the run leaves `awaiting_author` by any other route.
47. **GEA-FR-THNY** The draft is held in memory for the life of the running application, is written to no preference and no backend operation, and no draft of one run is ever read for another.
48. **GEA-FR-MRDK** A merge run's escalation is answered in the run region exactly as any run's escalation is: GEA-FR-BVMQ, GEA-FR-XTUE and GEA-FR-VWQH hold for it without change, and the draft is keyed by the run's id. A merge run's `Send answers` invokes `answer_graduation_escalation` once with the whole ordered set.
49. **GEA-FR-WQZH** A merge run's escalation additionally names the **affected paths** as `merge.unresolvedPaths` of the run, as project-relative paths rather than as a count, and the **publication choice** of `merge.publication`: the result is left uncommitted in the base worktree, or it is committed under the author's message. Both are read from the run and are read text on the terms of GEA-FR-UIEA. The message renders as escaped text.
50. **GEA-FR-HBCF** A merge run's escalation that the backend refuses with `merge_branch_moved` is rendered on the terms of GEA-FR-TEMG: the refusal says a branch moved and that nothing was written, every entered answer stays, and every question stays reachable.

## Non-functional requirements
- The dialog holds no state that survives the application, so nothing here needs migration and a relaunch loses only what was never sent.
- The whole surface is operable from the keyboard alone: paging, selecting a row, typing free text, and sending are all reachable, and focus is never trapped inside the dialog.
- Every text in the dialog meets the contrast floor in all three theme settings, and the measure and type size follow the reader's chosen font rather than a fixed pixel value.
- Answering is never blocked by a slow read: the questions render from the run record the section already holds.
