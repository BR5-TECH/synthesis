# Graduation escalation answering

**Spec code:** `GEA`

## Intent
Where the author answers work that has stopped to ask them something. A graduation run rests in `awaiting_author`, a merge run included, and a work stream update rests `escalated`, each carrying a reason and an ordered set of questions; this is the surface that takes the answers back. It renders the questions with the application's one question block (per `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md`), so an author who has answered a discussion agent already knows how to answer a run, and the two never look or behave differently. The questions are what the author reads. The reason is the agent's own account of why it stopped, and it stays one click away rather than in front of the questions. What the author has entered and not yet sent is held against the run's own questions, so paging, a re-read of the run, a switch to another run and back, and a refusal of the whole set all leave the work where it was. Out of scope: what raised the escalation and what the loop does with the answers, which are `../core/GRD-graduation.md`'s, `../ai/GRL-graduation-loop.md`'s and `../core/GRB-graduation-rebase.md`'s; the rail and the run region a run's dialog is rendered inside, which are `GRU-graduation-runs.md`'s; the window an update's is rendered inside, which is `WSS-work-stream-selector.md`'s; any judgement of the answers, since the set is carried back whole and unread; and the plan update of an apply run, which holds no escalation and is answered in the change discussion (per `../core/OPU-openspec-plan-updates.md` OPU-FR-QUST, `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` DQA-FR-XFEE).

## UI contract boundary
- **Owned by the UI**: the head of the question block — the turn that asked and the reason disclosure; the affected paths and the boundary a semantic-rebase escalation of an update names, and the affected paths and the publication choice a merge run's escalation names; how a proposed response maps onto an option of the question block; the entry each answer submits; the in-panel dialog's placement and measure; and the unsent answer draft — what it is keyed by, what it survives, and when it is dropped. Paging, answer rows, rails, the own answer, the note, what counts as answered, Submit, and the inline refusal belong to the question block (per `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` DQA-FR-HSBK).
- **Delegated to backend (abstract)**: `answer_graduation_escalation` — owned by `../core/GRD-graduation.md` (GXD-FR-BJYT) — and `"answer work stream update escalation (id, answers)"` — owned by `../core/WKS-work-streams.md` (WKS-FR-CBXW). Exactly one of them is invoked, once, with the whole ordered set. A run's escalation, a merge run's included, goes through the first; an update's goes through the second. This surface introduces no operation of its own, and reads what it renders from the operations `GRU-graduation-runs.md` and `WSS-work-stream-selector.md` already invoke.

## Functional requirements
1. **GEA-FR-QBWN** An escalation renders its questions with the question block of `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` (DQA-FR-HSBK, DQA-FR-RTWE). Paging, the answer rows, the rails, the own answer, the note, the counts, Submit, and the inline refusal follow that spec. This surface draws no question set of its own.
2. **GEA-FR-KMVR** The head of the block names the turn that asked, from the escalation's recorded origin: the work turn, the review turn, or the merge turn.
3. **GEA-FR-SXXB** The head carries a **"Why the run stopped"** disclosure that holds the escalation's `reason`, exactly as it was recorded (per `../tools/ESU-escalate-to-user-tool.md` ESU-FR-12). The disclosure is **closed by default**, and every question shows in full while it is closed.
    - *Why:* The reason can be a long account of the agent's work, and the author needs only the questions to answer.
4. **GEA-FR-JEOX** The disclosure and the reason are set at the chrome's caption size in the quieter text tone. The question text is set on the type scale of `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` DQA-FR-FJXQ.
5. **GEA-FR-ZRGP** An escalation a **semantic-rebase turn** raised additionally names the **affected paths** its record carries, as project-relative paths rather than as a count, together with the publication boundary the reconciliation stands at. A run's escalation reads both from the run (per `../core/GRD-graduation.md` GXD-FR-XPUR); an update's reads them from its update record (per `../core/WKS-work-streams.md` WKS-FR-ZKUP).
    - *Why:* A decision between two versions of a requirement is a decision about named files, and an author cannot take it without knowing which.
6. **GEA-FR-UIEA** Those paths are read text on the terms `GRU-graduation-runs.md` sets for every path that surface renders, and they open nothing. They stand in the block's context, between its head and the question.
7. **GEA-FR-DNBI** Each recorded proposed response — up to three per question — is one option of the question block: its `summary` is the option label, and its `description` is the option description that the rails show.
8. **GEA-FR-ZGHY** A question recorded with no proposed responses renders on the terms of `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` DQA-FR-ZNOQ: the own-answer field alone, open, with nothing to choose between.
9. **GEA-FR-BHKU** A proposed response's `description` is display-only and is submitted nowhere.
10. **GEA-FR-VWQH** Submitting invokes `"answer graduation escalation"` once with the whole ordered set, each entry naming the question's recorded position and its effective `{ answer, summary, note }` value, so the loop matches every answer to the question it was given for without reading a word of either.
11. **GEA-FR-TSQO** A chosen proposed response submits `{ answer: the response's answer, summary: the response's summary, note: the note text }`, the note left out where it is blank. The own answer submits `{ answer: the text, summary: its first 120 characters }` and no note.
12. **GEA-FR-NXFQ** The own answer is never held to a proposed response's summary format: its summary is a cut of its own text and nothing more.
13. **GEA-FR-PLTO** The note of a question is offered and submitted on the terms of `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` DQA-FR-ROXG, DQA-FR-JJON and DQA-FR-UAKC: only beside a chosen proposed response, never beside the own answer, and it answers nothing.
14. **GEA-FR-VIPR** Until the backend has accepted the set and published the updated run state, the run is still rendered as waiting on the author: no part of this surface shows it as resumed, running, or answered on the strength of the call having been made.
15. **GEA-FR-TEMG** A refusal renders inline at the foot of the block (per `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` DQA-FR-WKTP) with every entered answer and note intact and every question still reachable, so the author corrects what was refused rather than typing the set again.
16. **GEA-FR-RZQX** A model's proposed answer is **clarification metadata and never an answer**: this surface preselects no response from one, prefills no own-answer field from one, and treats a `Medium`, `Low`, or `Not confident` proposal as answering nothing.
17. **GEA-FR-QKDA** The author's effective answer is theirs to give and to change on every question.
18. **GEA-FR-NPUV** This surface renders no confidence indicator, that being a value a later UI contract may require and this one does not.
19. **GEA-FR-SKWP** It renders the internal field names of a proposed response nowhere — a response reads as its summary and its description and as nothing else.
20. **GEA-FR-BVMQ** A **run's** escalation, a merge run's included, is rendered inside the run region as an in-panel dialog belonging to the affected run: it is no floating overlay of the window and no application modal (per `SNV-shell-navigation.md` SNV-FR-56), it takes no focus trap, and the rest of the queue and the rest of the window stay operable while it stands.
20. **GEA-FR-HWRT** The block takes the presentation it has in the draft's discussion column (per `DDS-draft-discussion.md` DDS-FR-BRHN, DDS-FR-CVLM): the question stands above the answer group, the group is the card on the canvas tone, and the rows, the drawn radios, and the selected row take that column's treatment. Only the block's placement and measure are this surface's (GEA-FR-DPTT).
    - *Why:* The author meets the same questions in both places, so they must look the same in both.
21. **GEA-FR-DPTT** The block is held to a **measure** and centred in the region rather than filled to the region's width, and the measure is expressed as a multiple of the UI role's own size so it moves with the reader's chosen font (per `OVW-overview.md` OVW-FR-13).
    - *Why:* The run region has no conversation whose measure the block could take (per `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` DQA-FR-RDPU).
22. **GEA-FR-IHIZ** What the author has entered and not yet sent is an **unsent answer draft** held by this surface, and nothing short of sending it replaces it. It holds what `DQA-discussion-question-answering.md` DQA-FR-EGZS names: per question, the answer selected, the own words, and the note text.
23. **GEA-FR-XTUE** The draft is keyed by the run's id together with each question's recorded position — never by the question's text and never by the index of the page showing.
24. **GEA-FR-LWQD** This surface serves a **work stream update's** escalation on the same terms as a run's: it renders the recorded reason and question set from the update record, and sends the whole ordered set through the operation that owns that record. An update's unsent answer draft is keyed by the **stream's** id together with each question's recorded position, as GEA-FR-XTUE keys a run's.
25. **GEA-FR-BZQN** An update's escalation is rendered inside the update resolution window (per `WSS-work-stream-selector.md` WSS-FR-ZMPC) rather than in the run region, belonging to a stream and to no run (per `../core/GRB-graduation-rebase.md` GRB-FR-KZUN). A merge run's escalation is never rendered there.
26. **GEA-FR-QULY** The draft survives paging between questions, a re-list on `"graduation queue changed"`, a re-read on `"graduation run changed"`, the author selecting another run in the rail and coming back, a failed read that leaves the region empty (per `RUN-runs.md` RUN-FR-16), and a backend refusal of the whole set.
27. **GEA-FR-VBUG** A run re-read while an escalation stands is **reconciled rather than replaced**: the reason, the questions, and their proposed responses are redrawn from what came back, every draft entry whose position the re-read run still records is kept against that position, and a draft entry for a position the run no longer records is dropped with it.
28. **GEA-FR-KTME** The draft is discarded when the escalation it belongs to is answered and the backend has accepted the set, and when the run leaves `awaiting_author` by any other route.
29. **GEA-FR-THNY** The draft is held in memory for the life of the running application, is written to no preference and no backend operation, and no draft of one run is ever read for another.
30. **GEA-FR-MRDK** A merge run's escalation is answered in the run region exactly as any run's escalation is: GEA-FR-BVMQ, GEA-FR-XTUE and GEA-FR-VWQH hold for it without change, and the draft is keyed by the run's id. A merge run's **Submit** invokes `answer_graduation_escalation` once with the whole ordered set.
31. **GEA-FR-WQZH** A merge run's escalation additionally names the **affected paths** as `merge.unresolvedPaths` of the run, as project-relative paths rather than as a count, and the **publication choice** of `merge.publication`: the result is left uncommitted in the base worktree, or it is committed under the author's message. Both are read from the run and are read text on the terms of GEA-FR-UIEA. The message renders as escaped text.
32. **GEA-FR-HBCF** A merge run's escalation that the backend refuses with `merge_branch_moved` is rendered on the terms of GEA-FR-TEMG: the refusal says a branch moved and that nothing was written, every entered answer stays, and every question stays reachable.

## Non-functional requirements
- The dialog holds no state that survives the application, so nothing here needs migration and a relaunch loses only what was never sent.
- The whole surface is operable from the keyboard alone: the reason disclosure, paging, selecting a row, typing the own answer or a note, and submitting are all reachable, and focus is never trapped inside the dialog.
- Every text in the dialog meets the contrast floor in all three theme settings, and the measure and type size follow the reader's chosen font rather than a fixed pixel value.
- Answering is never blocked by a slow read: the questions render from the run record the section already holds.
